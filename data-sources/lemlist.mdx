    Multichannel sales outreach and cold email platform. Website: [lemlist.com](https://www.lemlist.com).

    **Connect**

    You connect lemlist with your **API Key**. Steps: [Connect a data source](/data-sources/connect).

    **Data views**

    A widget reads one data view: you pick it first, then its metrics and dimensions. lemlist has 1 data view:

  <Tab title="How it works">

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Connect lemlist">
        In the client's **Data sources**, open **lemlist**, name the connection and enter your **API Key**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Use it in widgets">
        Add a data widget, pick **lemlist**, then a data view, metrics and dimensions. One connection can be linked to many clients.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    **How the data arrives**

    Oviond asks lemlist for the numbers when a widget is added or changed, when you click **Refresh**, and on the report's automatic refresh. Nothing is copied ahead of time. PDFs print the data saved in the report.

    **When the connection stops working**

    If the details stop working (for example a key is deleted), the connection shows **Needs attention**. Click **Edit connection details**, enter them again and click **Save and test**. Fixing it fixes every client that uses it. See [Test Connection](/data-sources/test).
