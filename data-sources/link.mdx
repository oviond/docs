---
title: "Link to Client"
description: "Attach a connected data source to a specific client."
---

A connection lives at the **workspace** level — one login can be linked to many clients. Linking picks the account, property, or page for a client and makes that data source available in the client's widgets.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the data sources list">
    Open the client and click **Data sources** in the sidebar — or, from inside a report, click **Data Sources** in the editor sidebar. Click the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the connection">
    Under **Select Profile**, find a connection you've already made, or click **Add New** to make a new one — see [Connect](/data-sources/connect).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Click **Connect** on the connection. Oviond loads the available accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the account">
    Under **Select Account**, choose which ad account, property, or page maps to this client. See [Profiles](/data-sources/profiles).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The connection shows **Connected**, and every widget you build for this client can pull from it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Disconnect

On the client's **Data sources** page, open the platform and click **Disconnect** on the connected profile. The connection stays in your workspace — it's just no longer linked to this client. Widgets in this client's reports that use it stop returning data.

<Note>
  Disconnect takes effect at once, with no dialog to confirm. The connection stays in **Settings → Data sources** and can be linked to the same or a different client at any time.
</Note>
