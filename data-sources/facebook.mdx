  <Tab title="Overview">

    Facebook Pages analytics and insights. Website: [facebook.com](https://www.facebook.com).

    **Connect**

    You connect Facebook by signing in on the platform's website and letting Oviond read your data. Steps: [Connect a data source](/data-sources/connect).

    **Data views**

    A widget reads one data view: you pick it first, then its metrics and dimensions. Facebook has 6 data views:

    | Data view | ID | Metrics | Dimensions |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | Performance | `PERFORMANCE` | 100 | 3 |
    | Posts | `POSTS` | 35 | 11 |
    | Videos | `VIDEOS` | 48 | 20 |
    | Stories | `STORIES` | 7 | 7 |
    | Custom Metrics | `CUSTOM_METRICS` | 11 | 8 |
    | Events | `EVENTS` | 5 | 3 |

    Every metric and dimension, with its ID, format and summary, is in the **Metrics & dimensions** tab.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="How it works">

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Connect Facebook">
        In the client's **Data sources**, open **Facebook** and sign in to your Facebook account. Allow Oviond to read your data.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Pick the Facebook Page">
        Choose the Facebook Page to report on.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Use it in widgets">
        Add a data widget, pick **Facebook**, then a data view, metrics and dimensions. One connection can be linked to many clients.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    **How the data arrives**

    Oviond asks Facebook for the numbers when a widget is added or changed, when you click **Refresh**, and on the report's automatic refresh. Nothing is copied ahead of time. PDFs print the data saved in the report.

    **When the connection stops working**

    If the sign-in expires or access is removed, the connection shows **Needs attention**. Click **Refresh connection** and sign in again with the same account. Fixing it fixes every client that uses it. See [Test Connection](/data-sources/test).

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Limitations">

    **Limits in Oviond**

    Each field's own rules are in the **Metrics & dimensions** tab.

    - **Connect it in the app.** AI tools (the MCP server and Ask Oviond) can't connect Facebook, because it needs a sign-in in a browser. Once it is connected, they can link and use it.
    - **Some metrics and dimensions can't be used together.** The widget editor only offers the ones that work together.
    - **Some data views have advanced settings.** Under **Show Advanced Settings** in the widget's data settings you can change more options. Some settings take one choice, some take several.
    - **Deprecated fields are tagged.** Fields Facebook no longer supports are marked **Deprecated** in the **Metrics & dimensions** tab and noted in the changelog. A widget that uses one may show an error or no data.
