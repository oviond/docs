  <Tab title="Overview">

    Website uptime monitoring service. Website: [uptimerobot.com](https://uptimerobot.com).

    **Connect**

    You connect UptimeRobot with your **API Key**. Steps: [Connect a data source](/data-sources/connect).

    **Data views**

    A widget reads one data view: you pick it first, then its metrics and dimensions. UptimeRobot has 1 data view:

    | Data view | ID | Metrics | Dimensions |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | Uptime | `UPTIME` | 5 | 5 |

    Every metric and dimension, with its ID, format and summary, is in the **Metrics & dimensions** tab.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="How it works">

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Connect UptimeRobot">
        In the client's **Data sources**, open **UptimeRobot**, name the connection and enter your **API Key**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Pick the monitor">
        Choose the monitor to report on.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Use it in widgets">
        Add a data widget, pick **UptimeRobot**, then a data view, metrics and dimensions. One connection can be linked to many clients.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    **How the data arrives**

    Oviond asks UptimeRobot for the numbers when a widget is added or changed, when you click **Refresh**, and on the report's automatic refresh. Nothing is copied ahead of time. PDFs print the data saved in the report.

    **When the connection stops working**

    If the details stop working (for example a key is deleted), the connection shows **Needs attention**. Click **Edit connection details**, enter them again and click **Save and test**. Fixing it fixes every client that uses it. See [Test Connection](/data-sources/test).
