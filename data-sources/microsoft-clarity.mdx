  <Tab title="Overview">

    Session recording and heatmap analytics for understanding user behavior. Website: [clarity.microsoft.com](https://clarity.microsoft.com).

    **Connect**

    You connect Microsoft Clarity with your **API Token**. Steps: [Connect a data source](/data-sources/connect).

    **Data views**

    A widget reads one data view: you pick it first, then its metrics and dimensions. Microsoft Clarity has 2 data views:

    | Data view | ID | Metrics | Dimensions |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | Dashboard | `DASHBOARD` | 7 | 9 |
    | Ux Issues | `UX_ISSUES` | 12 | 5 |

    Every metric and dimension, with its ID, format and summary, is in the **Metrics & dimensions** tab.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="How it works">

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Connect Microsoft Clarity">
        In the client's **Data sources**, open **Microsoft Clarity**, name the connection and enter your **API Token**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Use it in widgets">
        Add a data widget, pick **Microsoft Clarity**, then a data view, metrics and dimensions. One connection can be linked to many clients.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    **How the data arrives**

    Oviond asks Microsoft Clarity for the numbers when a widget is added or changed, when you click **Refresh**, and on the report's automatic refresh. Nothing is copied ahead of time. PDFs print the data saved in the report.

    **When the connection stops working**

    If the details stop working (for example a key is deleted), the connection shows **Needs attention**. Click **Edit connection details**, enter them again and click **Save and test**. Fixing it fixes every client that uses it. See [Test Connection](/data-sources/test).

  </Tab>
