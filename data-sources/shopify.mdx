<Tabs sync={false}>
  <Tab title="Overview">

    Ecommerce platform for online stores. Website: [shopify.com](https://shopify.com).

    **Connect**

    You connect Shopify with your **Shop Domain**, **API Key** and **API Secret**. Steps: [Connect a data source](/data-sources/connect).

    **Data views**

    A widget reads one data view: you pick it first, then its metrics and dimensions. Shopify has 1 data view:

    | Data view | ID | Metrics | Dimensions |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | Sales | `SALES` | 221 | 228 |

    Every metric and dimension, with its ID, format and summary, is in the **Metrics & dimensions** tab.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="How it works">

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Connect Shopify">
        In the client's **Data sources**, open **Shopify**, name the connection and enter your **Shop Domain**, **API Key** and **API Secret**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Use it in widgets">
        Add a data widget, pick **Shopify**, then a data view, metrics and dimensions. One connection can be linked to many clients.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    **How the data arrives**

    Oviond asks Shopify for the numbers when a widget is added or changed, when you click **Refresh**, and on the report's automatic refresh. Nothing is copied ahead of time. PDFs print the data saved in the report.

    **When the connection stops working**

    If the details stop working (for example a key is deleted), the connection shows **Needs attention**. Click **Edit connection details**, enter them again and click **Save and test**. Fixing it fixes every client that uses it. See [Test Connection](/data-sources/test).

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Limitations">

    **Limits in Oviond**

    Each field's own rules are in the **Metrics & dimensions** tab.

    - **Not offered to accounts billed through the Shopify App Store.**
    - **Some metrics and dimensions can't be used together.** The widget editor only offers the ones that work together.
    - **Some data views have advanced settings.** Under **Show Advanced Settings** in the widget's data settings you can change more options. Some settings take one choice, some take several.
    - **Deprecated fields are tagged.** Fields Shopify no longer supports are marked **Deprecated** in the **Metrics & dimensions** tab and noted in the changelog. A widget that uses one may show an error or no data.
