  <Tab title="Overview">

    SEO and AI-visibility platform tracking brand mentions across AI assistants. Website: [seocrawl.ai](https://seocrawl.ai).

    **Connect**

    You connect SEOcrawl with your **API Token**. Steps: [Connect a data source](/data-sources/connect).

    **Data views**

    A widget reads one data view: you pick it first, then its metrics and dimensions. SEOcrawl has 4 data views:

    | Data view | ID | Metrics | Dimensions |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | Ai Visibility History | `AI_VISIBILITY_HISTORY` | 1 | 3 |
    | Ai Mentions | `AI_MENTIONS` | 2 | 1 |
    | Ai Share Of Voice | `AI_SHARE_OF_VOICE` | 3 | 3 |
    | Ai Prompts | `AI_PROMPTS` | 3 | 1 |

    Every metric and dimension, with its ID, format and summary, is in the **Metrics & dimensions** tab.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="How it works">

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Connect SEOcrawl">
        In the client's **Data sources**, open **SEOcrawl**, name the connection and enter your **API Token**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Pick the property">
        Choose the property to report on.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Use it in widgets">
        Add a data widget, pick **SEOcrawl**, then a data view, metrics and dimensions. One connection can be linked to many clients.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    **How the data arrives**

    Oviond asks SEOcrawl for the numbers when a widget is added or changed, when you click **Refresh**, and on the report's automatic refresh. Nothing is copied ahead of time. PDFs print the data saved in the report.

    **When the connection stops working**

    If the details stop working (for example a key is deleted), the connection shows **Needs attention**. Click **Edit connection details**, enter them again and click **Save and test**. Fixing it fixes every client that uses it. See [Test Connection](/data-sources/test).

  </Tab>
