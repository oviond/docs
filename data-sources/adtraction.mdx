  <Tab title="Overview">

    Affiliate marketing platform for tracking clicks, conversions, and commission across partner programs. Website: [adtraction.com](https://adtraction.com).

    **Connect**

    You connect Adtraction with your **API Token**, **Market** and **Currency**. Steps: [Connect a data source](/data-sources/connect).

    **Data views**

    A widget reads one data view: you pick it first, then its metrics and dimensions. Adtraction has 3 data views:

    | Data view | ID | Metrics | Dimensions |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | Partner Statistics | `PARTNER_STATISTICS` | 11 | 3 |
    | Partner Programs | `PARTNER_PROGRAMS` | 11 | 2 |
    | Advertiser Statistics | `ADVERTISER_STATISTICS` | 11 | 3 |

    Every metric and dimension, with its ID, format and summary, is in the **Metrics & dimensions** tab.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="How it works">

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Connect Adtraction">
        In the client's **Data sources**, open **Adtraction**, name the connection and enter your **API Token**, **Market** and **Currency**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Use it in widgets">
        Add a data widget, pick **Adtraction**, then a data view, metrics and dimensions. One connection can be linked to many clients.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    **How the data arrives**

    Oviond asks Adtraction for the numbers when a widget is added or changed, when you click **Refresh**, and on the report's automatic refresh. Nothing is copied ahead of time. PDFs print the data saved in the report.

    **When the connection stops working**

    If the details stop working (for example a key is deleted), the connection shows **Needs attention**. Click **Edit connection details**, enter them again and click **Save and test**. Fixing it fixes every client that uses it. See [Test Connection](/data-sources/test).

  </Tab>
