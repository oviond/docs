---
title: "Test Connection"
description: "Check that a connection still works, and fix it when it needs attention."
---

Every connection shows its health in one of two words:

- **Working** — the connection is fine and data should flow.
- **Needs attention** — the login expired or was revoked, or the stored details stopped working. Widgets that use it can show old data or none.

Oviond checks every connection once a day, and again whenever it tries to refresh a login, so the status updates on its own. You can also test a connection yourself at any time.

## Test a connection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Data sources">
    The table lists every connection with its **Status**. Use the status filter to show only the ones marked **Needs attention**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the connection">
    Click the row, or **Manage**. A panel opens with the connection's details: **Authentication** (OAuth 2.0 or API key), **Linked clients**, **Connected** and **Last tested**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Test">
    Oviond tries the connection now. You see **Connection is working**, or **Connection failed** with the reason.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fix a connection that needs attention

- **Sign-in platforms (OAuth)** — click **Refresh connection** and sign in again with the same account. Your account picks stay as they were.
- **API-key platforms** — click **Edit connection details**, enter all the details again, and click **Save and test**. For safety, stored details are never shown.

Fixing a connection fixes it for every client and widget that uses it — you don't need to link each client again.

If it still fails, the person who connected it may have lost access in the platform. Check their access there, or connect again with an account that has access.

<Note>
  Over the API, `POST /v1/datasources/test` takes an `auth_id` and a `datasource_id` and returns `connected` plus a `message`. The result is saved on the connection, so its status in the app shows the test.
</Note>
