  <Tab title="Overview">

    Cloud-based customer support software that helps businesses manage customer interactions across email, phone, chat, and other channels. Website: [freshdesk.com](https://www.freshdesk.com).

    **Connect**

    You connect Freshdesk with your **API Key** and **Domain**. Steps: [Connect a data source](/data-sources/connect).

    **Data views**

    A widget reads one data view: you pick it first, then its metrics and dimensions. Freshdesk has 2 data views:

    | Data view | ID | Metrics | Dimensions |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | Tickets | `TICKETS` | 5 | 7 |
    | Tickets Data | `TICKETS_DATA` | 4 | 8 |

    Every metric and dimension, with its ID, format and summary, is in the **Metrics & dimensions** tab.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="How it works">

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Connect Freshdesk">
        In the client's **Data sources**, open **Freshdesk**, name the connection and enter your **API Key** and **Domain**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Use it in widgets">
        Add a data widget, pick **Freshdesk**, then a data view, metrics and dimensions. One connection can be linked to many clients.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    **How the data arrives**

    Oviond asks Freshdesk for the numbers when a widget is added or changed, when you click **Refresh**, and on the report's automatic refresh. Nothing is copied ahead of time. PDFs print the data saved in the report.

    **When the connection stops working**

    If the details stop working (for example a key is deleted), the connection shows **Needs attention**. Click **Edit connection details**, enter them again and click **Save and test**. Fixing it fixes every client that uses it. See [Test Connection](/data-sources/test).

  </Tab>
