  <Tab title="Overview">

    Google Analytics 4 property data. Website: [analytics.google.com](https://analytics.google.com).

    **Connect**

    You connect Google Analytics 4 (GA4) by signing in on the platform's website and letting Oviond read your data. Steps: [Connect a data source](/data-sources/connect).

    **Data views**

    A widget reads one data view: you pick it first, then its metrics and dimensions. Google Analytics 4 (GA4) has 2 data views:

    | Data view | ID | Metrics | Dimensions |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | Performance | `PERFORMANCE` | 57 | 140 |
    | Custom Metrics | `CUSTOM_METRICS` | 1 | 3 |

    Every metric and dimension, with its ID, format and summary, is in the **Metrics & dimensions** tab.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="How it works">

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Connect Google Analytics 4 (GA4)">
        In the client's **Data sources**, open **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)** and sign in to your Google Analytics 4 (GA4) account. Allow Oviond to read your data.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Pick the Google Analytics account">
        Choose the Google Analytics account.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Pick the property">
        Choose the property to report on. The list shows the ones inside the Google Analytics account you picked.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Use it in widgets">
        Add a data widget, pick **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)**, then a data view, metrics and dimensions. One connection can be linked to many clients.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    **How the data arrives**

    Oviond asks Google Analytics 4 (GA4) for the numbers when a widget is added or changed, when you click **Refresh**, and on the report's automatic refresh. Nothing is copied ahead of time. PDFs print the data saved in the report.

    **When the connection stops working**

    Oviond renews the Google Analytics 4 (GA4) sign-in by itself. If the sign-in expires or access is removed, the connection shows **Needs attention**. Click **Refresh connection** and sign in again with the same account. Fixing it fixes every client that uses it. See [Test Connection](/data-sources/test).

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Limitations">

    **Limits in Oviond**

    Each field's own rules are in the **Metrics & dimensions** tab.

    - **Connect it in the app.** AI tools (the MCP server and Ask Oviond) can't connect Google Analytics 4 (GA4), because it needs a sign-in in a browser. Once it is connected, they can link and use it.
    - **Some metrics and dimensions can't be used together.** The widget editor only offers the ones that work together.
    - **Deprecated fields are tagged.** Fields Google Analytics 4 (GA4) no longer supports are marked **Deprecated** in the **Metrics & dimensions** tab and noted in the changelog. A widget that uses one may show an error or no data.
