  <Tab title="Overview">

    Project management platform for client work: tasks and time tracking. Website: [teamwork.com](https://www.teamwork.com).

    **Connect**

    You connect Teamwork with your **API Key** and **Site**. Steps: [Connect a data source](/data-sources/connect).

    **Data views**

    A widget reads one data view: you pick it first, then its metrics and dimensions. Teamwork has 2 data views:

    | Data view | ID | Metrics | Dimensions |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | Tasks | `TASKS` | 11 | 3 |
    | Time | `TIME` | 3 | 3 |

    Every metric and dimension, with its ID, format and summary, is in the **Metrics & dimensions** tab.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="How it works">

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Connect Teamwork">
        In the client's **Data sources**, open **Teamwork**, name the connection and enter your **API Key** and **Site**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Pick the project">
        Choose the project to report on.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Use it in widgets">
        Add a data widget, pick **Teamwork**, then a data view, metrics and dimensions. One connection can be linked to many clients.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    **How the data arrives**

    Oviond asks Teamwork for the numbers when a widget is added or changed, when you click **Refresh**, and on the report's automatic refresh. Nothing is copied ahead of time. PDFs print the data saved in the report.

    **When the connection stops working**

    If the details stop working (for example a key is deleted), the connection shows **Needs attention**. Click **Edit connection details**, enter them again and click **Save and test**. Fixing it fixes every client that uses it. See [Test Connection](/data-sources/test).

  </Tab>

    **Limits in Oviond**

    Each field's own rules are in the **Metrics & dimensions** tab.

    - **Some metrics and dimensions can't be used together.** The widget editor only offers the ones that work together.
