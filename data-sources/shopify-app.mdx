---
title: "Install from the Shopify App Store"
description: "Add Oviond to your Shopify store, connect the store to a client, and pay through Shopify."
---

You can install Oviond straight from the Shopify App Store. It connects your store as a data source and, if you don't already pay Oviond by card, bills you through Shopify.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Install Oviond from the Shopify App Store and approve it in Shopify.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in or sign up">
    Oviond opens. Sign in, or create an account and finish the short [setup](/onboarding/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a client">
    On **Finish Integrating Shopify**, choose the client the store belongs to (**Select a client...**) and click **Connect**. You see *Shopify connected successfully!*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan on Shopify">
    If your account isn't already paying by card, Oviond sends you to Shopify to choose a plan. Your billing page then shows **Billed through Shopify**. See [Plans](/billing/plans#billed-through-shopify).
  </Step>
</Steps>

The store is now linked to that client, so Shopify widgets show its data. To use the store for another client, link it from that client's **Data sources** page — see [Link to Client](/data-sources/link).

<Note>
If you see *Authorization failed*, install the app again from the Shopify App Store.
</Note>
