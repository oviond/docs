Data sources are connections between Oviond and your clients' marketing platforms. Once connected, Oviond pulls live data from those platforms into your reports and dashboards — no manual exports or copy-pasting required. Each data source is linked on a per-client basis, so the data in a report always belongs to the client that report is built for.

## How data sources work

When you connect a data source, Oviond guides you through an OAuth flow — or, for some platforms, an API-key form — where you authorize Oviond to read your data. Oviond stores the result as a reusable **connection** (an auth profile).

After connecting, you link a specific account, property, or page (for example, a particular Google Ads account or GA4 property) to a client. That linked data source then becomes available when you build widgets for that client.

### Connections (profiles)

A connection represents one authorized credential for a platform — typically tied to one login. If you manage multiple clients' accounts through a single login (such as a Google Ads manager account), one connection can cover all of those sub-accounts.

You can reuse the same connection across multiple clients. For example, if your workspace has a single Google Ads manager account containing sub-accounts for 10 clients, you authorize Google Ads once and then link each client to their respective sub-account — without re-authenticating.

## Listing your connections

To see every connection across your workspace, go to **Settings → Data sources** (`/settings/data-sources`). The table has a row per connection: **Data source**, **Profile**, **Clients** (how many use it), **Status** and **Connected** (the date). Search by name, or use **Filters** to show one status or one platform. Click a row, or **Manage**, to open the connection's panel. The **Connect** button adds a new one.

To see the data sources linked to one client, open that client and click **Data sources** in the sidebar. Platforms the client is already connected to sort to the top, each card reading **Connected**.

## Checking connection status

Every connection shows **Working** or **Needs attention**. Oviond checks them every day on its own, and you can click **Test** in a connection's panel at any time. To fix one that needs attention, see [Test Connection](/data-sources/test).

## Disconnecting and deleting

**Disconnecting** removes a data source from one client but keeps the connection for reuse. **Deleting** a connection removes it from your workspace and from every client that used it.

### Disconnect a data source from a client

1. Open the client and go to its **Data sources** page.
2. Open the platform.
3. Click **Disconnect** on the connected profile.

The connection stays available and can be linked to the same or a different client at any time.

### Delete a connection

<Warning>
  Deleting a connection removes it for good and may affect widgets that use it. Widgets that relied on it stop showing data. This can't be undone.
</Warning>

1. Go to **Settings → Data sources** and click the connection.
2. Click **Delete profile**.
3. Type `DELETE` to confirm, then click **Delete profile**.
