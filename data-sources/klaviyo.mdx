title: "Klaviyo"
description: "How Klaviyo data is synced into Oviond, and its limits."
---

Klaviyo is a **synced** data source. Oviond doesn't ask Klaviyo for data each time a report opens. A background sync copies your Klaviyo data into Oviond, and your widgets read that copy. This keeps reports fast and stays within Klaviyo's request limits.

## How syncing works

- **Widgets show only what's synced.** A day that hasn't synced yet shows `0`. A table with nothing synced for its dates shows **No data yet for these dates**.
- **History:** Oviond keeps the last **24 months** (730 days). Dates older than that show no data.
- **Newest first:** the first sync loads the most recent days first, so recent reports fill in long before the full history is loaded.
- **Sync frequency:** you pick it when you link a client: every hour, 3 hours, 6 hours, 12 hours, or once a day. When several clients use the same Klaviyo connection, it syncs at the fastest frequency any of them picked.

## Connect Klaviyo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your API key">
    In the client's **Data sources**, open **Klaviyo**, name the connection and paste a Klaviyo private API key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the Revenue metric">
    Choose the metric Klaviyo counts conversions and revenue with — usually **Placed Order**. Klaviyo's own email and SMS events (sent, opened, clicked) aren't offered, because they aren't conversions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the sync frequency and save">
    Choose how often to sync and click **Save**. Saving starts the first sync straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Klaviyo's limits

- **Report requests:** Klaviyo allows **2 a minute and 225 a day**. Campaign and flow reports use them, so the first full load of a big account can take a while.
- **Campaign reports** refresh at most **every 6 hours**, to stay within the daily limit. The sync re-checks the last 30 days of campaigns, because their stats keep changing after a send.
- **Flows and event counts** can refresh every hour.
- **Email campaigns only.** SMS campaigns aren't included in campaign reports.
- **Unique counts** (for example unique opens) are counted per day, so over several days they can run slightly high.

## Changing the Revenue metric

Changing the Revenue metric **changes the sync**:

- The sync **stops** loading campaign and flow data for the old Revenue metric — unless another client using the same connection still picks it — and **starts** loading it for the new one from the beginning.
- The data already loaded for the old one is **kept**. If you switch back, the sync carries on from where it stopped.
- If a sync is already running, it picks up the change within a few minutes. You don't need to start a new one.

Other Klaviyo data (event counts, campaign and flow lists) doesn't depend on the Revenue metric and keeps syncing as normal.

## Sync History

Open the Klaviyo connection and click **Sync History** to see:

- **Status:** Synced, Syncing, Syncing history (with how far it has got), Waiting for provider limits, Sync problem, or Paused.
- **Actions** (admins only): **Sync now** gets the latest days, **Pause** / **Resume** stops or restarts all syncs, and **Re-sync** loads the whole history again.
- **Recent syncs:** the last 50 syncs, with how many rows each one saved.

## When something goes wrong

- **Klaviyo refuses or limits requests:** the sync waits and tries again on its own. Your reports keep showing the data already synced.
- **The sync keeps failing:** after 3 failures in a row, you get one notification. Check the API key in the connection.
- **A sync is interrupted** (for example a server restart): the next sync carries on from where it stopped.

## Unlink or delete

- **Unlinking a client** keeps the connection and its synced data. Linking again carries on from where it stopped.
- **Deleting the connection** deletes all of its synced data. Connecting again loads the full history from the start.
