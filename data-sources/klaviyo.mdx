  <Tab title="Overview">

    Email marketing and automation platform. Website: [klaviyo.com](https://www.klaviyo.com).

    **Connect**

    You connect Klaviyo with your **API Key**. Oviond copies Klaviyo data on a schedule, and widgets show what the last sync stored. Steps: [Connect a data source](/data-sources/connect).

    **Data views**

    A widget reads one data view: you pick it first, then its metrics and dimensions. Klaviyo has 3 data views:

    | Data view | ID | Metrics | Dimensions |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | Metric Aggregates | `METRIC_AGGREGATES` | No fixed list | 16 |
    | Campaigns Report | `CAMPAIGNS_REPORT` | 27 | 9 |
    | Flows Report | `FLOWS_REPORT` | 27 | 4 |

    Every metric and dimension, with its ID, format and summary, is in the **Metrics & dimensions** tab.

    **Limits in Oviond**

    Each field's own rules are in the **Metrics & dimensions** tab.

    - **Data is as fresh as the last sync.** Widgets show what the last sync stored. A day not synced yet shows `0`.
    - **Some metrics and dimensions can't be used together.** The widget editor only offers the ones that work together.
