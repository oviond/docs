---
title: "Close, hide or turn off Ask Oviond"
description: "Close the Ask Oviond chat, or turn Ask Oviond off for everyone in your account so the chat and its message bar are hidden."
---

Ask Oviond is the AI chat built into Oviond. It sits in a message bar at the bottom of the app and in the report editor. You can close the chat at any time, or an admin can turn Ask Oviond off for the whole account.

## Close the chat

The chat closes without stopping a reply. The answer keeps coming while it's closed.

- Click the **X** at the top of the chat.
- Press **Esc**.
- Click anywhere outside the chat.

The message bar stays at the bottom of the page. To hide the bar too, turn Ask Oviond off.

## Turn Ask Oviond off

Turning it off hides the chat and the message bar for everyone in your account. Use this if your team doesn't want the chat, for example if you use Oviond from your own AI app (Claude, ChatGPT and others) through the [MCP server](/mcp/overview) instead.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Ask Oviond settings">
    Go to **Settings → Ask Oviond**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn the switch off">
    Turn off the **Ask Oviond** switch at the top of the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The change applies to everyone in your account straight away. Teammates may need to refresh the page.

<Note>
Only an account admin can change this switch. Other users can see it but can't change it.
</Note>

What stays when Ask Oviond is off:

- Your past chats, the log of questions on **Settings → Ask Oviond**, and your balance.
- Oviond's [MCP server](/mcp/overview). Turning Ask Oviond off does not turn off MCP access. MCP access has its own switch in **Settings → Security**.

## Turn Ask Oviond back on

Go to **Settings → Ask Oviond** and turn the **Ask Oviond** switch on. The chat and the message bar come back for everyone in your account.

<Tip>
If Ask Oviond is missing but its switch is on, check **Settings → Security**. Ask Oviond uses MCP access, so it is hidden while MCP access is off.
</Tip>
