title: "Balance and costs"
description: "How Ask Oviond is paid for: your balance, adding money, and the log of every question."
---

Ask Oviond is pay as you go. Each question uses a little money from your account's **Ask Oviond balance** — what it costs depends on how much work the answer took. A short question costs a few cents; building a whole report from a file costs more.

Click the balance in the chat to see how much you have left.

## Add money

Click the balance in the chat, then **Add money**. Choose an amount and pay by card. Money you add **never expires**. Each top-up gets a paid invoice, listed with your plan's invoices in **Settings → Billing → Invoice history**.

Only people who manage billing can add money. Others see *Ask your account owner to add money.*

## What you're charged for

- An answer is charged when it ends.
- An answer that **failed** — an error, or nothing said or done — is **free**.
- If you **Stop** an answer, you pay for the work done up to that point.
- A question starts only while your balance is above $0. An answer is never cut short: if it costs more than what was left, the balance goes a little below $0 and the next money you add covers it.

When the balance runs out, the chat shows *Your balance is used up*, with a button to add money.

## The log

**Settings → Ask Oviond** lists every question, the tools each one used (failed ones in red) and what it cost.

- Admins see every row in the account, but a teammate's question shows as private.
- Everyone else sees their own questions.
- Deleting a chat removes its questions from the log; the cost stays, shown as *Chat deleted*.

The log only shows questions asked in Ask Oviond. AI apps you connect through the [MCP server](/mcp/overview) (Claude, ChatGPT and others) don't appear here and don't use this balance; what they change shows in the [Activity log](/activity-log/overview) with the source **mcp**.

The same page has the switch to [turn Ask Oviond off](/ask-oviond/turn-on-off).
