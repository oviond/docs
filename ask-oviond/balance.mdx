Each question uses part of your account's Ask Oviond allowance. How much depends on how much work the answer took: a short question uses a little; building a whole report from a file uses more.

## Paid and trial plans

Your plan includes Ask Oviond usage. On a paid plan it comes back on the 1st of each month. A trial gets it once.

When it runs out, the chat shows *You've reached your Ask Oviond limit*, with the date it resets on a paid plan. You can ask again once it resets. To get more before then, contact support.

## Lifetime deals

On a lifetime deal, Ask Oviond is pay as you go, from your account's **Ask Oviond balance**. Click the balance in the chat to see how much you have left.

### Add money

Click the balance in the chat, then **Add money**. Choose an amount and pay by card. Money you add **never expires**. Each top-up gets a paid invoice, listed with your plan's invoices in **Settings → Billing → Invoice history**.

Only people who manage billing can add money. Others see *Ask your account owner to add money.*

When the balance runs out, the chat shows *Your balance is used up*, with a button to add money.

## What counts

- A question counts when its answer ends.
- An answer that **failed** — an error, or nothing said or done — is **free**.
- If you **Stop** an answer, the work done up to that point counts.
- A question starts only while you have some left. An answer is never cut short: if it uses more than what was left, the next amount you get covers it.

## The log

**Settings → Ask Oviond** lists every question and the tools each one used (failed ones in red). On a lifetime deal it also shows what each question cost.

- Admins see every row in the account, but a teammate's question shows as private.
- Everyone else sees their own questions.
- Deleting a chat removes its questions from the log; the row stays, shown as *Chat deleted*.

The log only shows questions asked in Ask Oviond. AI apps you connect through the [MCP server](/mcp/overview) (Claude, ChatGPT and others) don't appear here and don't use your Ask Oviond allowance; what they change shows in the [Activity log](/activity-log/overview) with the source **mcp**.

The same page has the switch to [turn Ask Oviond off](/ask-oviond/turn-on-off).
