**Ask Oviond** is the AI chat built into Oviond. Ask it a question about your clients, reports or marketing data, or ask it to do the work for you — create a client, build a dashboard, add widgets, change a date range, make a PDF or schedule an email.

It works in your own account, as you. It can do exactly what you can do in the app, and nothing more.

## Where to find it

- **The message bar** at the bottom of the app. Click it or start typing, and the chat opens above it. It isn't shown on **Settings** pages.
- **The Ask Oviond tab** in the report editor's sidebar. It knows which report, page and widgets you're looking at, so you can say "this report" or "this widget".

The chat suggests a few questions to start, based on the page you're on.

## What it can do

- **Answer questions** about your account and your numbers, for example *"How did Acme's Google Ads do last month?"* It knows today's date, so "last month" and "this quarter" just work.
- **Build and change things**: clients, reports, dashboards, pages, widgets, templates, themes, data connections, PDFs and scheduled emails.
- **Build a report from a file.** Add a PDF, image, spreadsheet, Word or PowerPoint file and ask it to copy it. See [Files](/ask-oviond/files).
- **Answer how-to questions** from Oviond's docs and help centre, with links to the pages it used. Links open in a new tab.
- **Make PDFs.** When a PDF is ready, the answer shows a **Download PDF** button.
- **Get you to a person.** Ask for support and it shows a button to chat with our support team.

<Note>
Ask Oviond only helps with Oviond and marketing reporting. It politely says no to anything else — poems, general questions, code and so on.
</Note>

## Risky actions need your OK

Before Ask Oviond archives, deletes, removes or unlinks anything, or sends email, it shows an approval card that says exactly what will change. Nothing happens until you click **Approve**. See [Approvals](/ask-oviond/approvals).

## Stop an answer

Click **Stop** in the message bar to stop an answer. What it already wrote stays, marked *You stopped this answer*. Anything it already did stays done.

## Your chats

- Under each answer, **Copy** copies the text. In the report editor, **Add as text widget** puts the answer on the report as a Text widget.
- Past chats are listed in **Chat history**. You can rename, share or delete them, one or many at a time.
- **Share** gives a teammate in your account a read-only link to the chat. Chats are never public.
- Deleting a chat also deletes the files added to it.

## What it costs

Each question uses a little money from your account's Ask Oviond balance. See [Balance and costs](/ask-oviond/balance).

## Hide it

Close the chat with the **X**, the **Esc** key, or by clicking outside it. An admin can also turn Ask Oviond off for the whole account. See [Close, hide or turn off Ask Oviond](/ask-oviond/turn-on-off).

<Tip>
Prefer your own AI app? Claude, ChatGPT and others can work in Oviond through the [MCP server](/mcp/overview). In the chat, click **+** → **Use Oviond in** and pick ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Cursor or **All AI connectors…** to see the setup steps for that app.
</Tip>
