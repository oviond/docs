---
title: "Approvals"
description: "Ask Oviond asks before it archives, deletes or sends anything."
---

Some actions are hard to undo or reach other people. Ask Oviond never runs them on its own. It shows an **approval card** first, and waits for you.

These always need your approval:

- Archiving, deleting, removing or unlinking anything.
- Creating, changing, turning on or testing a scheduled email (automation), and sending email.
- Inviting a team member, and other account changes such as turning off API access.

## What the card shows

- **What it will do** — for example *Archive clients*.
- **Which records, by name** — one name, or for several at once, the count (for example *3 items*) and every name in a list. A long list shows the first 10 and *and N more*.
- **What happens next**:
  - *Archive* actions: the records go to the [Archive](/archive/view). You can restore them from **Settings → Archive**.
  - *Delete* and *remove* actions: *This can't be undone.*

A card for several records at once is **red**, and its button says **Approve all N**, so it can't be mistaken for a single change.

## Approve or decline

- **Approve** runs the action, and Ask Oviond carries on with the rest of your request.
- **Decline** cancels it. Nothing changes, and Ask Oviond tells you so.

<Note>
When you ask Ask Oviond to "delete" a client, report or most other things, it **archives** them. The card says so, and you can restore them later.
</Note>
