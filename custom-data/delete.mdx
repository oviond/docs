---
title: "Archive custom data"
description: "Archive an uploaded dataset. You can restore it later."
---

Archiving a dataset moves it to the [Archive](/archive/view). Widgets that use it stop showing its data until you restore it or point them at another dataset.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage Custom Data">
    From a **Custom Data** widget, click **Manage** next to the **Dataset** label.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Archive">
    On the dataset's row, choose **Archive** and confirm in the **Archive Dataset** dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To bring it back, go to **Settings → Archive**, find the dataset and click **Restore**. Deleting it from the Archive removes it for good.

## API

`DELETE /v1/custom-data/{id}` archives the dataset. It returns `404` if the dataset doesn't exist in your account.
