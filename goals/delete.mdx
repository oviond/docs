---
title: "Archive a goal"
description: "Archive a goal you no longer need. You can restore it later."
---

Archiving a goal moves it to the [Archive](/archive/view). You can restore it from there at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Goals tab">
    In the report editor, open the **Goals** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archive the goal">
    On the goal's row, click **Archive**, then confirm in the **Archive Goal** dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Widgets that show the archived goal stop showing its progress until you restore it or point them at another goal.
</Warning>

To bring it back, go to **Settings → Archive**, find the goal and click **Restore**.

## API

`DELETE /v1/goals/{id}` archives a goal.
