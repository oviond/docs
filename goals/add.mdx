---
title: "Add a goal"
description: "Define a target for a metric and track progress against it."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report">
    From the client's **Reports** tab, open the report or dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Goals tab">
    In the editor sidebar, open the **Goals** tab and click **Add Goal**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the data">
    Under **Data**, choose the data source, the metric and, if you want, a dimension. Only sources linked to this client are listed. Click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the goal">
    - **Target** — the number to reach.
    - **Type** — **Fixed value** compares the metric with the target as it is; **% change vs previous** compares the change against the previous period.
    - **Comparison** — what counts as reaching the goal: **At least (≥)**, **Exactly (=)** or **At most (≤)**.
    - **Format** — how the value is shown: Numeric, Decimal, Percentage or Currency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and save">
    Enter a **Goal name** (for example, *Q4 Revenue Target*) and, if you like, a **Description**. Click **Save Goal**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To show the goal in a report, add a **Goal** widget from **Add Content** and pick the goal.

## API

`POST /v1/goals` creates a goal.
