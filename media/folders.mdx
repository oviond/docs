---
title: "Folders"
description: "Group your media into folders."
---

Folders keep your **Media Library** tidy. Each file is in one folder at a time, or in none. The folder list is on the left of the **Library** tab.

## Open a folder

Click a folder to see only its files. Click **All files** to see every file again. **Search media…** searches inside the open folder.

## Create a folder

Click **New folder** at the bottom of the folder list, type a name, and click **Create**. The folder is added to the list.

## Upload into a folder

Open the folder, then switch to the **Upload** tab. The footer shows **Uploading to** and the folder name. New files go into that folder.

A cropped copy of an image goes into the same folder as the image.

## Move files into a folder

- **One file:** open the tile's `⋯` menu and click **Move to folder**. Pick the folder and click **Confirm**.
- **Many files:** tick two or more tiles, then click **Move** in the footer.

Moving a file takes it out of its old folder.

To take a file out of its folder, open its `⋯` menu and click **Remove from folder**. The file stays in **All files**.

## Rename or delete a folder

Hover a folder, open its `⋯` menu, and click **Rename** or **Delete**.

<Note>
Deleting a folder **does not delete its files**. They stay in **All files**, in no folder.
</Note>

## Who can manage folders

Everyone can open folders and upload into them. Creating, renaming and deleting folders, and moving files, needs permission to edit shared assets. View-Only users don't see these buttons.

## API

- `GET /v1/media/folders` lists your folders, A to Z.
- `POST /v1/media/folders` with a `name` creates a folder and returns its `id`. The name is trimmed and can be up to 100 characters.
- `PUT /v1/media/folders/{id}` with a new `name` renames a folder. Returns `404` if the folder isn't in your account.
- `DELETE /v1/media/folders/{id}` deletes a folder. Its files stay, in no folder.
- `PUT /v1/media/group` with `ids` and `folder_id` moves files into that folder. Send `folder_id: null` to take them out of every folder.
- `GET /v1/media?folder_id=…` lists the files in one folder.
- `POST /v1/media/presigned-url` takes an optional `folder_id` to upload straight into a folder.
