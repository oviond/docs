After your first sign-in, Oviond opens a short setup screen, **Let’s set up your Oviond workspace**. It asks for three things and takes less than a minute. Until you finish it, other pages bring you back here.

## The setup screen

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Your name** | Your name as it appears in the app and in the activity log. |
| **Company name** | Your agency or company name. |
| **Company website** | Your website, for example `www.acme.com`. If you leave off `https://`, Oviond adds it. Oviond uses it to set up your first demo report. |

Click **Continue to your Dashboard** when all three are filled in.

## What Oviond sets up

While you wait, you see **Building your starter dashboard…**. Oviond then:

- Saves your name, company name and website.
- Adds Oviond's starter themes to your account, and picks up your website's icon for your agency branding.
- Creates your **first client**: your own company, using the name and website you entered. It uses your browser's timezone and US dollars. You can change both later in the client's settings.
- Adds a starter dashboard to that client, built from the **Digital Marketing Dashboard** template. It shows demo numbers until you connect your data sources.

When it's done, you land on the **Clients** page, where you can see your first client.

<Tip>
Want to report on a real client straight away? Click **Add Client** on the Clients page. See [Add a client](/clients/add).
</Tip>

## If setup fails

If Oviond can't finish, you see **We couldn't finish setup** with a **Retry** button. Click **Retry**. What you typed is kept, so you don't need to type it again.

You can change your name later under [My account](/account/overview), and your company details under [Company](/company/overview).
