---
title: "Update theme"
description: "Edit an existing theme's colours, fonts, or styling tokens."
---

Editing a theme changes its visual tokens. Report data isn't affected — only the look and feel of reports that use the theme.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report's Themes panel">
    In the report editor sidebar, open the **Themes** panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the theme editor">
    Hover the theme row and click **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the tokens">
    Update any of: name, **Primary colour**, **Palette mode** and individual chart colours, canvas colours, **Headings**, **Header background**, **Radius**, **Shadow**, and **Font**. You can also edit the cover-page and thank-you-page designs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview">
    The report canvas previews your changes live as you edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where it applies and save">
    Set **Apply to** (Current report, This client's reports, or All reports), then click **Save and apply**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
If you change the **Primary colour** without overriding the chart palette, Oviond re-derives the ten chart colours from the new primary. Open **Edit chart colours** and set them by hand if you need specific colours pinned.
</Tip>

<Note>
**Headings** colours heading widgets independently of body text. Leave it unset and headings follow the general **Text** colour.
</Note>

## Cover and thank-you pages

Under **Report pages**, the theme sets how a Multi-Page Report's **Cover page** and **Thank you** page look.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Visible by default** | Which parts show on new reports: **Artwork** (cover only), **Client logo**, **Agency logo**, **Heading** and **Subheading**. |
| **Default heading** / **Default subheading** | The text new reports start with. Type `/` to insert a variable, such as the client name or the date range. |
| **Heading colour** / **Subheading colour** | The text colours on the page. **Use theme colour** goes back to the theme's own colour. |
| **Cover artwork** | Pick a generated cover design, or **Upload custom cover image** to use your own picture. Remove the custom image to go back to a generated design. |

Each report can still turn parts on or off, and change the text, in its own cover and thank-you pages.

<Note>
To make a copy instead of changing the original, hover the theme row and choose **Duplicate**. Editing themes needs **Admin access** or **Client access**.
</Note>
