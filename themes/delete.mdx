---
title: "Archive theme"
description: "Archive a theme you no longer need. You can restore it later."
---

Archiving a theme removes it from your account's Themes panel and moves it to the [Archive](/archive/view). Reports aren't changed — a report that used the theme falls back to a default theme the next time it opens.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report's Themes panel">
    In the report editor sidebar, open the **Themes** panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archive the theme">
    Hover the theme row and click **Archive**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm in the **Archive theme** dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To bring it back, go to **Settings → Archive**, find the theme and click **Restore**.

<Warning>
If the archived theme was your account **default**, or a **Client theme**, those fall back to a default theme.
</Warning>

<Note>
Archiving themes needs **Admin access** or **Client access**.
</Note>
