---
title: "Delete theme"
description: "Remove a theme from your account."
---

Deleting a theme removes it from your account's Themes panel. The reports themselves aren't touched — a report that referenced the deleted theme falls back to a default theme on its next render.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report's Themes panel">
    In the report editor sidebar, open the **Themes** panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete the theme">
    Hover the theme row and click the **Delete** (trash) action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the **Delete theme** dialog, confirm. Deleting a theme can't be undone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
If the deleted theme was your account **default**, or was set as a **Client theme**, those references fall back to a default theme. Already-styled reports keep their own theme until you re-apply another one.
</Warning>

<Note>
Deleting themes requires permission to manage shared assets.
</Note>
