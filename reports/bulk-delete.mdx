---
title: "Bulk Archive"
description: "Archive several reports at once."
---

Archive several reports at once when you clean up — for example, old drafts, or a client you stopped working with.

<Note>
Archived reports go to the [Archive](/archive/view) with all their pages, widgets and automations. You can restore them from there at any time. Permanent deletion only happens when you delete an item from the Archive, and it can't be undone.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the client's Reports tab">
    Open the client whose reports you want to clean up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the reports">
    Tick the checkbox on each report you want to archive. A bar appears at the bottom with the number selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archive">
    Click **Archive**, then confirm in the **Archive Reports** dialog. The reports and everything under them move to the Archive.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
You can only archive reports for clients you're allowed to edit. Read-only users and users who only see other clients can't archive them.
</Note>
