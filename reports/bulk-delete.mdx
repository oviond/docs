---
title: "Bulk Delete"
description: "Remove multiple reports at once."
---

Bulk delete speeds up cleanup when you're archiving a batch of old reports — for example, rolling over to a new fiscal year or removing drafts after a client offboards.

<Warning>
Deleted reports move to the [Archive](/archive/view) along with all their pages, widgets, and automations. You can restore them from the Archive at any time, or permanently delete them there. Permanent deletion is irreversible — export anything you need first.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the client's Reports tab">
    Open the client whose reports you want to clean up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter selection mode">
    Select the reports you want to remove using the report cards' checkboxes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the reports">
    Check every report you want to delete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete">
    Confirm the deletion. The selected reports and everything under them move to the Archive.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Each report you delete must belong to a client you have permission to edit. Read-only users and users scoped to other clients can't delete it.
</Note>
