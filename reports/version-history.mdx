---
title: "Version history"
description: "See earlier versions of a report, preview them, and restore one."
---

Oviond saves your report as you work, and every change is recorded as a version. **Version History** lets you look back and bring an older version back.

## See and restore a version

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Version History">
    In the report editor sidebar, open **Version History**. Changes are listed newest first, grouped by day (**Today**, **Yesterday**, then dates), with who made each one and when.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview a version">
    Click a change. The report shows how it looked then, marked **Previewing**. Nothing changes yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restore it, or go back">
    Click **Restore Version** to make it the current report, or **Exit Preview** to go back without changing anything.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Restoring is recorded as a new change, so you can go back again if you need to.

<Tip>
For small slips while editing, **Undo** (Ctrl/⌘ + Z) is faster. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/reports/keyboard-shortcuts).
</Tip>
