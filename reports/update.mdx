---
title: "Update Report"
description: "Rename a report and change its settings, date range, page setup and sharing."
---

A report's settings are in three places in the report editor:

| Where | What you change there |
|---|---|
| **Settings** tab | Name, description, logo source, automatic data refresh, and (dashboards only) section links. |
| **Pages** tab (Multi-Page Reports) | Cover page, table of contents, thank-you page, paper size, orientation, page header and page numbers. |
| **Share** dialog | The public link, password, PDF download and date selector. See [Share a report](/reports/share). |

The date range is in the report header.

## Settings tab

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report">
    From the client's **Reports** tab, open the report you want to change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Settings tab">
    In the editor sidebar, open **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report details">
    Change the **Report name** and add a short **Report description** so your team knows what the report is for. The name appears on the shared link, in PDF exports and in the email subject of automations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Logo source">
    Under **Use logos from**, choose **Client** or **Agency**. It starts with the source set in the client's settings; choosing one here changes it for this report only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Data refresh">
    Turn on **Refresh data automatically** to keep connected widgets up to date, and pick the **Refresh schedule**: **Hourly** or **Daily**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dashboard navigation (dashboards only)">
    Turn on **Show section links** to add quick links so viewers can jump between the dashboard's sections.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Changes save with the report, like every other edit.

## Date range

Use the date picker in the report header to set the report's date range, for example *Last 30 Days*. Every widget that uses the report's date follows it, and connected widgets fetch fresh data. It saves straight away; you don't need to be in edit mode.

A date range can't go past today. Some data sources, like Microsoft Ads, refuse future dates, and all the widgets on a report share one range. The same rule applies to the API, MCP and Ask Oviond.

## Pages tab (Multi-Page Reports)

- Turn the **Cover page**, **Table of contents** and **Thank you page** on or off.
- Under **Document settings**:
  - **Page setup**: **Paper size** (A4 or Letter) and **Orientation** (Portrait or Landscape). Changing the orientation moves widgets to fit the new page size.
  - **Page header**: show the **Logo**, **Report title** and **Date range** at the top of each page.
  - **Page numbers**: show a number on each page.

The cover and thank-you pages take their look (artwork, heading and colours) from the report's [theme](/themes/update).

## PDF download

Whether viewers of the shared link may download a PDF is the **Allow PDF Download** switch in the **Share** dialog. See [PDF Export](/automations/pdf).

<Note>
A report belongs to the client you create it under, and it can't be moved to a different client. To use the same layout for another client, save it as a [template](/templates/add) and create the report from that template under the other client.
</Note>
