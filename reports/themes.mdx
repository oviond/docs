---
title: "Design Report Themes"
description: "Create visual themes in Oviond to apply consistent colors, fonts, and design tokens across all your client reports and dashboards."
---

Themes in Oviond let you define a reusable set of visual design tokens — color palettes, font choices, and related styling — that you can apply across your reports and dashboards. Instead of styling each project individually, you create a theme once and apply it wherever you need consistent branding.

## What is a theme?

A theme is a named collection of design settings:

- **Color palette** — primary, secondary, and accent colors used in charts, KPI tiles, and backgrounds.
- **Font choices** — the typefaces used for headings and body text within your reports.

When you apply a theme to a project, all widgets and pages in that project inherit the theme's design settings. Updating the theme later does not automatically propagate to projects that have already been assigned it — you need to reapply the theme to see changes in existing projects.

<Note>
  Themes control the visual style of your report content. For branding that appears in the report frame itself — such as your agency logo, custom domain, or client portal colors — see your [white-label branding settings](/white-label/overview).
</Note>

## Create a new theme

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Themes library">
    Click **Themes** in the left sidebar (or find it under **Brand** or **Design** depending on your sidebar layout).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Theme">
    Click the **+ New Theme** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the theme">
    Enter a name that reflects the theme's purpose or the client it's designed for — for example, "Client A Brand Colors" or "Agency Dark Theme".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure colors and fonts">
    Set your color palette and font choices (see the sections below). You can also do this after the theme is created by editing it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the theme">
    Click **Save**. The theme appears in your Themes library and is available to apply to any project.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit theme colors and fonts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Themes library">
    Click **Themes** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the theme">
    Click the theme you want to edit, or select **Edit** from its three-dot menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the color palette">
    In the **Colors** section, update each color swatch by entering a hex color value or using the color picker. Common palette slots include:

    - **Primary color** — the dominant accent used in charts and KPI tiles.
    - **Secondary color** — a supporting accent for multi-series charts.
    - **Background color** — the page background for the report canvas.
    - **Text color** — the default color for labels and widget text.

    Add additional colors to the palette if your charts display multiple data series.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose fonts">
    In the **Fonts** section, select the typeface for **Headings** and **Body text** from the available font options. Font choices affect all text rendered within the report pages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save** to update the theme.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use your client's brand hex codes and typefaces to create a per-client theme. This keeps every report you produce for that client visually on-brand without manual styling each time.
</Tip>

## Apply a theme to a project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the project">
    From the **Reports** screen, click the project you want to style.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open project settings">
    Click **Project Settings** from the project menu or the settings icon in the editor toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a theme">
    In the **Theme** dropdown, choose the theme you want to apply to this project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save**. All pages and widgets in the project now use the selected theme's colors and fonts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Applying a theme overrides any widget-level color customizations that were set individually. If you need one widget to use a different color, adjust it manually after applying the theme.
</Note>

## Duplicate a theme

Duplicating a theme is the fastest way to create a variation — for example, a light and dark version of the same brand palette, or a starting point for a similar client.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Themes library">
    Click **Themes** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the theme menu">
    Hover over the theme card and click the three-dot menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Duplicate">
    Click **Duplicate**. Oviond creates a copy with "Copy of" prepended to the theme name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rename and edit the duplicate">
    Open the duplicate theme and update its name and color or font settings as needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delete a theme

<Warning>
  Deleting a theme does not remove the styling from projects that currently use it — those projects retain the last-applied colors and fonts. However, the deleted theme will no longer be available to apply to future projects, and you won't be able to make centralized updates to it.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Themes library">
    Click **Themes** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the theme menu">
    Hover over the theme card and click the three-dot menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Delete">
    Click **Delete**, then confirm when prompted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How themes interact with white-label branding

Themes and white-label branding settings serve different purposes in Oviond:

| Setting | Controls |
|---|---|
| **Theme** | Colors and fonts used inside report pages — chart colors, KPI tile styling, background and text colors. |
| **White-label branding** | The report frame visible to clients — your agency logo, custom domain, client portal header and footer colors. |

Use themes to match the visual style of each report's content to your client's brand. Use white-label settings to ensure the report delivery experience reflects your agency's brand.

<Tip>
  For a fully branded client experience, set up your white-label settings once at the account level, then create per-client themes to style each report's content independently.
</Tip>
