Share a report with a client, stakeholder, or prospect via a public URL. The recipient opens the link in any browser — **no Oviond account required** — and sees the report with live data.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report">
    From the client's **Reports** tab, click the report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Share">
    The **Share** button sits in the top-right of the editor and opens the Share panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the link">
    On the **Link** tab, click **Copy** to copy the share URL. The link points to `/shared/{short-id}` on your share host.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make sure the link is active">
    Under **Sharing Options**, keep **Link is Active** on so anyone with the link can view the report. Turn it off to disable the link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: protect or extend it">
    Toggle **Enable Password** and set a passphrase, **Allow PDF Download** to let viewers export a PDF, and **Show Date Selector** to let viewers change the date range.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Share tabs

The Share panel has four tabs:

- **Link** — copy the public link, configure sharing options, and see **Report analytics**. See [Share link analytics](#share-link-analytics).
- **Email** — send the report now: choose who it's **From**, add **Recipients**, pick an email template or write a **Custom message**, then **Send via Email**. To send it on a schedule, use an [automation](/automations/add).
- **Download** — generate a PDF (available when **Allow PDF Download** is on). A Multi-Page Report prints page by page; a dashboard prints as one long page.
- **Embed** — copy an `<iframe>` snippet to embed the report in another site.

## Share link analytics

On the **Link** tab, under **Sharing Options**, the **Report analytics** card (**Client opens** — who opened the share link and what they did) shows **Last from:** and the country of the newest visit, when it is known.

Click **See analytics** to see more about the last 30 days in the same panel. Click the back arrow to return. It has two parts:

**Overview** adds up all visits:

- **Views** — how many times the link was opened. Opening it again within 30 minutes counts as the same view.
- **Visitors** — how many different people (browsers) opened it.
- **Avg. time per view**, **PDF downloads** (PDFs visitors downloaded from the link), and when the link was **Last viewed**.
- **Views by day** — a chart of views for each day.
- **Pages** — how many times each page was viewed, and how long people stayed on it.
- **Devices** and **Countries** — desktop, mobile or tablet, and where people opened the link from.

**Visits** lists every visit, newest first, grouped by day. Each visit shows the time, the device, the country, how many pages, how long, and how many actions. Click a visit to see what that person did, step by step: each page they opened and for how long, and each action — a click on a button or link, **Present**, a new date range, **Refresh** or a PDF download. Click **Show more** to see older visits.

On a link with a password, the visit starts with **Password screen**, then each click on **Unlock**: **Clicked Unlock: wrong password** or **Clicked Unlock: right password**, then the report's pages. The visit shows how many wrong passwords there were. Someone who never gets past the password shows in **Visits** as **Stopped at the password screen**, but is not counted as a view. The password they typed is never saved.

What is not counted:

- **People signed in to Oviond.** Only visitors who are not signed in are counted. Oviond can only see a sign-in when the link opens on the Oviond app's own address. On your share address (your custom domain, or the default share domain), your team's visits are counted like anyone else's.
- **A visit that never gets past the password**, in the views, the chart and the reports list. It shows in **Visits** only.
- **PDFs and scheduled emails.** Their pages are made by Oviond, not opened by a person.

We don't save visitors' IP addresses. The country comes from the visitor's network, and the device from their browser. When you delete a report for good, its analytics are deleted too.

The client's **Reports** tab also shows each report's **Views (30 days)** and **Last viewed**. See [List reports](/reports/list).

## Custom domain

Share links never expose the Oviond app domain. The link host resolves in order: the client's custom domain, then your account's default custom domain, then an unbranded platform fallback. Set up a [Custom Domain](/custom-domains/list) to serve links from your own white-label host. Your logo, favicon, and brand colors render on the share page out of the box.

## What your client sees

- **A password, if you set one.** The link asks them to **Enter password** and click **Unlock**. After too many wrong tries it asks them to wait a minute.
- **The report, with your branding.** A Multi-Page Report has a list of its pages to jump between; a dashboard can show section links (turn on **Show section links** in the report's [Settings](/reports/update)).
- **A date picker**, if you turned on **Show Date Selector**. Their changes are only for them — your report isn't changed.
- **A PDF button**, if you turned on **Allow PDF Download**. The PDF shows the report as it was last saved.
- **Present** — shows the report full screen, for meetings.
- If a widget can't load its data, they see *Data temporarily unavailable* instead of the technical reason. See [Widget messages](/widgets/troubleshooting).

## Live vs snapshot

Public links render live data every time they're opened. To send a static snapshot (e.g. end-of-month), use the **Download** tab or [PDF Export](/automations/pdf) instead.

<Warning>
Anyone with the link can view the report while **Link is Active** is on. Enable a password if the report contains sensitive client data.
</Warning>
