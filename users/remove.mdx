---
title: "Remove a user"
description: "Revoke a team member's access to your Oviond account."
---

Removing a user immediately revokes their access. Their login is deleted and they can no longer sign in to the account.

Only users with **Admin access** can remove team members.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Users">
    Go to **Settings → Users**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the user you want to remove">
    Find the user in the list and open their options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove the user">
    Choose to remove them, then confirm when prompted. The user is notified that they've been removed, and their access ends immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens to their work

Removing a user doesn't delete the clients or reports in your account — that content belongs to the account, not the individual user, so it stays in place and remains accessible to your other team members.

<Warning>
  You can't remove yourself, and you can't remove the account owner. Transfer ownership before attempting to remove an owner.
</Warning>
