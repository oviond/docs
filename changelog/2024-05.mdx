### Google Analytics 4 renames purchaser conversion rate

Google renamed the GA4 Data API metric `purchaserConversionRate` to `purchaserRate` on May 6, 2024. The old name no longer returns data. It is marked **Deprecated** in the widget editor.

- Purchase Conversion Rate (`purchaserConversionRate`)
