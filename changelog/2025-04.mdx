<Update label="2025-04-21" description="Instagram retires impressions and Reel plays">

### Instagram retires impressions and Reel plays

Meta removed Instagram impressions and Reel plays on April 21, 2025, and moved to **Views**. These metrics no longer return data. They are marked **Deprecated** in the widget editor.

- Profile Impressions (`account_impressions`)
- Impressions (`media_impressions`)
- Reel Plays (`reels_plays`)

</Update>
