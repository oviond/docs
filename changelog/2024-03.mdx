<Update label="2024-03-14" description="Facebook Pages retires Page Insights metrics">

### Facebook Pages retires page views, check-ins and engagement metrics

Meta removed a large group of Page Insights metrics on March 14, 2024. These metrics no longer return data. They are marked **Deprecated** in the widget editor.

- Mobile Check-ins (`page_places_checkin_mobile`)
- Unique Mobile Check-ins (`page_places_checkin_mobile_unique`)
- Section by Other (`page_views_by_offers`)
- Section by Home (`page_views_by_home`)
- Section by Posts (`page_views_by_posts`)
- Section by About (`page_views_by_about`)
- Section by Photos (`page_views_by_photos`)
- Section by Reviews (`page_views_by_reviews`)
- Total People Who Viewed (`page_views_logged_in_unique`)
- Logged Section by Other (`page_views_by_offers_unique`)
- Logged Section by Home (`page_views_by_home_unique`)
- Logged Section by Posts (`page_views_by_posts_unique`)
- Logged Section by About (`page_views_by_about_unique`)
- Logged Section by Photos (`page_views_by_photos_unique`)
- Logged Section by Offers (`page_views_by_reviews_unique`)
- Get Directions Clicks (`page_get_directions_clicks_by_site_logged_in_unique`)
- Phone Number Clicks (`page_call_phone_clicks_by_site_logged_in_unique`)
- Page Likes by Source (Your Page) (`page_fans_by_your_page`)
- Page Likes by Source (Restored Likes) (`page_fans_by_reactivated_accounts`)
- Page Likes by Source (Search) (`page_fans_by_search`)
- Page Likes by Source (Other) (`page_fans_by_other`)
- Page Likes by Source (Ads) (`page_fans_by_ads`)
- Page Likes by Source (Page Suggestions) (`page_fans_by_page_suggestions`)
- Engaged Users (`page_engaged_users`)
- Positive Actions (`page_positive_feedback`)
- Unique Positive Actions (`page_positive_feedback_unique`)
- Page Consumptions (`page_consumptions`)
- Content Stories (`page_content_activity`)
- Post Paid Impressions by Page Fans (`post_impressions_fan_paid`)
- Post Paid Reach by Page Fans (`post_impressions_fan_paid_unique`)

</Update>
