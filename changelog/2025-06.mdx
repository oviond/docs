<Update label="2025-06-15" description="Facebook Pages retires reach metrics">

### Facebook Pages retires reach metrics

Meta removed the Page and post reach metrics by June 15, 2025, and moved to **Viewers**. These metrics no longer return data. They are marked **Deprecated** in the widget editor.

- Total Reach (`page_impressions_unique`)
- Paid Reach (`page_impressions_paid_unique`)
- Viral Reach (`page_impressions_viral_unique`)
- Non-Viral Reach (`page_impressions_nonviral_unique`)
- Total Reach Of Posts (`page_posts_impressions_unique`)
- Paid Reach Of Posts (`page_posts_impressions_paid_unique`)
- Organic Reach Of Posts (`page_posts_impressions_organic_unique`)
- Viral Reach Of Posts (`page_posts_impressions_viral_unique`)
- Non-Viral Reach Of Posts (`page_posts_impressions_nonviral_unique`)
- Total Impressions Of Posts (`page_posts_impressions`)
- Paid Impressions Of Posts (`page_posts_impressions_paid`)
- Viral Impressions Of Posts (`page_posts_impressions_viral`)
- Non-Viral Impressions Of Posts (`page_posts_impressions_nonviral`)
- Post Reach (`post_impressions_unique`)
- Post Paid Reach (`post_impressions_paid_unique`)
- Post Reach by Page Fans (`post_impressions_fan_unique`)
- Post Organic Reach (`post_impressions_organic_unique`)
- Post Reach (Viral) (`post_impressions_viral_unique`)
- Post Reach (Non Viral) (`post_impressions_nonviral_unique`)

</Update>
