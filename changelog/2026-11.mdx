<Update label="2026-11-25" description="Data source pages and links from the widget editor">

### A docs page for every data source

Every data source now has its own page in [Data Sources](/data-sources/list): how you connect it, how it works, its limits, and every metric and dimension, with a search box and a menu of data views.

### Links from the widget editor

Hover a metric or dimension in the widget editor, then click the book icon to open its data source's page. On a **Deprecated** one, click the red note to see in the changelog when the vendor retired it.

### Retired metrics in the changelog

Metrics and dimensions that vendors retired are now listed in this changelog on the day each vendor retired them, back to 2023. Fields with no vendor date are listed under [17 July 2026](/changelog/2026-07#fields-retired-with-no-vendor-date).

### Instagram Profile Visits in Meta Ads

Meta Ads has a new metric, **Instagram Profile Visits**. It counts how many times people visited your Instagram profile after interacting with your ad, for example by clicking its call-to-action button, profile picture or username. It's in the **Clicks** group of the Account, Campaigns, Ad Sets and Ads views. See [Meta Ads](/data-sources/meta-ads).

</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-23" description="Metric details on hover, and filter rules">

### See what a metric means before you pick it

In the widget editor, hover a metric or dimension to see what it means, its format with an example number, how its total is worked out, and its ID with a copy button.

### Filters only use the widget's own fields

A filter's field must be one of the widget's metrics or dimensions. To filter on another dimension, add it to the widget first. The API, MCP and Ask Oviond now follow this rule too, so they no longer save filters the editor can't show. See [Configure a widget](/widgets/configure).

</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-19" description="Instagram daily metrics, GA4 and Google Sheets fixes">

### Instagram account metrics by day, month or year

The Instagram **Performance** view now shows account numbers by day, month or year: Followers, New Followers, Followers Lost, Net Followers, Posts, Reels, Posted Content, Likes, Comments, Saves, Shares, Reposts, Engagement, Accounts Engaged, Post Engagements, Reels Engagements, Views, Reach, Unique Reach and Visits. Profile Views works again, as **Visits**. New Followers now works for date ranges older than 30 days. Your widgets keep working; their metric IDs didn't change.

### Data source fixes

- **Google Analytics 4:** the account picker now lists every account, not just the first 200.
- **Google Sheets:** the folder picker shows only folders that hold a spreadsheet, so large Drives no longer load forever.
- **Google Sheets:** a column you pick gets the **Text** format right away, so **Set Data** no longer fails with "Choose symbol for …".

</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-18" description="Date ranges can end in the future">

### Date ranges can end in the future

A date range can now end after today, for example a season from 1 Sep to 31 Aug. Widgets show data up to today, and each day one more day is added, so you never have to change the dates. A range that hasn't started yet shows no data. This replaces the rule from 17 November that a range can't go past today. See [Date range](/reports/update#date-range).

### Dates follow the client's timezone

"Today" and every day in a chart now always follow the client's timezone (if the client has none, the account's, then UTC). In the API, `timezone` is no longer taken on `POST /v1/data/query` or `POST /v1/widgets/refresh`, and MCP `query_data` and `refresh_widgets` no longer have it: if you send it, it's ignored. See [Querying data](/api/concepts/querying-data).

</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-17" description="Share link analytics, media folders, dimension names and more">

### See who opens your share links

A report's share link now shows who opened it and what they did. On the **Link** tab, the **Report analytics** card shows the newest visit. Click **See analytics** for the last 30 days: views, visitors, time per view, PDF downloads, pages, devices, countries, and every visit step by step. See [Share link analytics](/reports/share#share-link-analytics).

### Folders in the media library

Put your images in folders. Create, rename and delete folders, move files into a folder or take them out, and uploads go into the folder you have open. See [Folders](/media/folders).

### Rename dimensions

The **Display** tab has a new **Dimension Settings** section. Give a dimension your own name, for example **Channel** instead of **Source / Medium**. Tables show it as the column header. See [Configure a widget](/widgets/configure).

### Filter and sort goals and calculated metrics

In the report editor, the **Goals** and **Calculated Metrics** panels now have **Filter** (by data source) and **Sort** (**Newest**, **Oldest**, **Name A-Z**, **Name Z-A**), like the **Assets** panel. See [Goals](/goals/overview) and [Calculated metrics](/calculated-metrics/overview).

### Date ranges can't go past today

The API, MCP and Ask Oviond now follow the same rule as the date picker: a date range can't include a day after today. Some data sources, like Microsoft Ads, refuse future dates, and all the widgets on a report share one range. See [Date range](/reports/update#date-range).

### Data source fixes

- **Active Campaign:** the **Contacts** view has a new **Contact Tags** dimension, and you can show only the contacts with one tag.
- **Instagram:** posts and reels are now picked separately in the advanced settings. A post or reel that was deleted, or isn't available any more, shows in red, so you can remove it.
- **LinkedIn Pages and LinkedIn Ads:** widgets work again. LinkedIn turned off the API version we used on 16 November.

### Views left the reports list

The client's **Reports** list no longer has the **Views** and **Last viewed** columns. You find who opened a report in its share link's analytics.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-15" description="Agency tools removed">

### Agency tools is gone

**Agency tools** is no longer in the sidebar, and you can't make a report without a client any more, in the app or the API. Create every new report under a client.

You can't open or edit old agency reports in the app any more. They keep working for the people you share them with: their shared links, PDFs and scheduled emails still work.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-15" description="Widgets no longer land on top of each other">

### New widgets and copies find free space

- **Free Canvas:** a widget you add with a click or the **+** menu goes into free space on the page, so it never covers another widget. If the page is full, it goes on the next page that has room, or on a new page. Dragging is still free.
- **Duplicate on Free Canvas:** copies keep their size and arrangement and go into free space, below the originals when there is room. If the page is full, they go on the next page with room, or on a new page.
- **The editor opens the new page.** After a widget or copy goes to another page, or you click **New Page** on **Page is full**, the editor takes you there. Your next widget goes on that page, not back on page 1.
- **Grid pages:** a new widget goes into the first free space that fits it, such as the space next to a half-width image. Before, it could land below the page and not save.
- **Duplicate on Grid:** if the copies don't fit on the page, nothing is copied. For one widget you see **Page is full**. For several, Oviond tells you to add a page or duplicate them one at a time.

See [Arrange & resize widgets](/widgets/layout) and [Duplicate widgets](/widgets/duplicate).

</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-12" description="Ask Oviond: clearer approvals, an on/off switch, and more">

### Ask Oviond is clearer and easier to control

- **Approval cards say exactly what will change** — the names of the records and how many. A card for several records is red, with **Approve all N**. Archive cards say you can restore from the Archive; delete cards say it can't be undone. See [Approvals](/ask-oviond/approvals).
- **Turn Ask Oviond on or off** for your whole account in **Settings → Ask Oviond**. Off hides the chat and its message bar. See [Close, hide or turn off Ask Oviond](/ask-oviond/turn-on-off).
- **Stop is just a stop.** A stopped answer now reads *You stopped this answer*, not an error.
- **Links you can click.** Links open in a new tab, docs links go to the page, and a ready PDF shows a **Download PDF** button.
- **It knows today's date**, so "last month" and "this quarter" work.
- **Oviond topics only.** Ask Oviond helps with Oviond, marketing reporting and the docs and help centre, and politely says no to anything else.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-11" description="Ask Oviond: pay as you go">

### Pay only for what you use

[Ask Oviond](/ask-oviond/overview), the AI chat built into Oviond, now runs on a balance. Each question uses a little money, based on how much work the answer took. Failed answers are free.

- Add money by card. Added money never expires, and each top-up gets a paid invoice in **Settings → Billing**.
- **Settings → Ask Oviond** lists every question, the tools it used and what it cost.
- Delete many chats at once from **Chat history**.

See [Balance and costs](/ask-oviond/balance).

</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-05" description="A note under the PDF button">

### Know which numbers a PDF uses

The **Download** tab now explains which numbers a PDF uses, so a PDF that doesn't match the screen is no surprise. See [PDF export](/automations/pdf).

</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-04" description="PDFs print the saved report">

### PDFs are faster and match the report

PDFs now print the data saved in the report, instead of fetching it again while printing. They're faster, and they match what you see in the report. If numbers look old, refresh the report before making the PDF.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-03" description="LinkedIn Ads, Search Console and X Ads fixes">

### Fixes for LinkedIn Ads, TikTok, Search Console and X Ads

- **LinkedIn Ads** shows member company names, and has two new dimensions: impression device type and placement name.
- **TikTok** Average Time Watched and Full Video Watched Rate are averaged, not added up, in totals. Average Time Watched shows as a time.
- **LinkedIn Ads**, **Google Search Console** and **X Ads** widgets read their account picks correctly again.

</Update>
