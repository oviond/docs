<Update label="2026-11-12" description="Ask Oviond: clearer approvals, an on/off switch, and more">

- **Approval cards say exactly what will change** — the names of the records and how many. A card for several records is red, with **Approve all N**. Archive cards say you can restore from the Archive; delete cards say it can't be undone. See [Approvals](/ask-oviond/approvals).
- **Turn Ask Oviond on or off** for your whole account in **Settings → Ask Oviond**. Off hides the chat and its message bar. See [Close, hide or turn off Ask Oviond](/ask-oviond/turn-on-off).
- **Stop is just a stop.** A stopped answer now reads *You stopped this answer*, not an error.
- **Links you can click.** Links open in a new tab, docs links go to the page, and a ready PDF shows a **Download PDF** button.
- **It knows today's date**, so "last month" and "this quarter" work.
- **Oviond topics only.** Ask Oviond helps with Oviond, marketing reporting and the docs and help centre, and politely says no to anything else.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-11" description="Ask Oviond: pay as you go">

### Pay only for what you use

[Ask Oviond](/ask-oviond/overview), the AI chat built into Oviond, now runs on a balance. Each question uses a little money, based on how much work the answer took. Failed answers are free.

- Add money by card. Added money never expires, and each top-up gets a paid invoice in **Settings → Billing**.
- **Settings → Ask Oviond** lists every question, the tools it used and what it cost.
- Delete many chats at once from **Chat history**.

See [Balance and costs](/ask-oviond/balance).

</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-05" description="A note under the PDF button">

The **Download** tab now explains which numbers a PDF uses, so a PDF that doesn't match the screen is no surprise. See [PDF export](/automations/pdf).

</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-04" description="PDFs print the saved report">

PDFs now print the data saved in the report, instead of fetching it again while printing. They're faster, and they match what you see in the report. If numbers look old, refresh the report before making the PDF.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-11-03" description="LinkedIn Ads, Search Console and X Ads fixes">

- **LinkedIn Ads** shows member company names, and has two new dimensions: impression device type and placement name.
- **TikTok** Average Time Watched and Full Video Watched Rate are averaged, not added up, in totals. Average Time Watched shows as a time.
- **LinkedIn Ads**, **Google Search Console** and **X Ads** widgets read their account picks correctly again.

</Update>
