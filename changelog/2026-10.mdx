<Update label="2026-10-30" description="Theme heading colours, Google Ads filters, TikTok thumbnails">

### Colour your cover and thank-you headings

A theme can now set the **Heading colour** and **Subheading colour** of the cover and thank-you pages. Find them under **Report pages** in the theme editor. See [Update theme](/themes/update).

### Google Ads: filter ad groups and ads by campaign

The **Ad Groups** and **Ads** data views can now be filtered by campaign and by ad group.

### TikTok: video thumbnails

TikTok widgets now show each video's thumbnail as a preview image.

### LinkedIn Ads: one row per campaign, group and creative

LinkedIn Ads tables now show one row per campaign, campaign group or creative.

### Fixes

- SE Ranking: search engine names read correctly again.
- Preview dimensions show their text in charts, sorting and filters.
- Canvas templates open in the canvas editor.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-26" description="New data sources: Paddle and Appfigures">

### Report on Paddle and Appfigures

- **Paddle** — transactions, subscriptions, customers and adjustments.
- **Appfigures** — App Store and Google Play keyword rankings, for your app and your competitors.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-23" description="Assets filter finds every asset">

### Filter assets by calculated metric or goal

The **Data Sources** filter in the **Assets** panel now finds assets built from calculated metrics and goals, and you can filter by **Calculated Metrics** or **Goals**.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-23" description="One message after connecting">

### No extra messages after you connect

Connecting a data source no longer shows extra "Unable to refresh" messages after it succeeds.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-22" description="TikTok Ads GMV Max">

### Report on TikTok Shop GMV Max campaigns

TikTok Ads has six new data views for GMV Max: **GMV Max**, **Product Campaigns**, **Products**, **Creatives**, **LIVE Campaigns** and **Livestreams**. In **Advanced Settings**, pick the TikTok Shops, campaigns and products to report on.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-22" description="Add saved items from the + menu">

### Add saved items without leaving the canvas

Select a widget and click **+** to add a saved **Asset**, **Calculated Metric** or **Goal** right below it. Choose **Start from Blank** for an empty widget.

**Custom Data** now has its own panel in the sidebar, next to Calculated Metrics and Goals.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-22" description="Advanced Settings opens when needed">

### Advanced Settings opens for you

If **Set Data** needs a setting from **Advanced Settings**, such as a TikTok GMV Max campaign or a Semrush region, the panel now opens for you.

</Update>

### Instagram Ads and Custom Data fixes

<Update label="2026-10-21" description="Spacing lines on report pages">

### See the spacing while you move widgets

When you drag or resize a widget on a report page, red lines show the gaps to its neighbours and to the page edges. Matching gaps are marked too.

Hold **Option** (Mac) or **Alt** (Windows) to turn off snapping and measure the distances.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-13" description="Widgets tell you why they failed">

### What went wrong, and how to fix it

When a widget can't load its data, it now says why — an expired connection, no access to the account, a setting the platform doesn't support, or the platform being busy — and shows the one button that fixes it: **Reconnect**, **Edit widget** or **Retry**.

People viewing a shared report or PDF see a calm "Data temporarily unavailable" instead.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-06" description="Client preview image">

### Choose the image a client shows

Client settings has a new **Preview image** section. It's the picture on the client's card in your Clients list and on shared link previews.

Upload your own from the media library, remove it, or refresh the screenshot of the client's website. The preview in settings is cropped exactly like the card on the Clients list, so what you see is what you get. Refreshing the website screenshot later replaces an uploaded image.

</Update>
