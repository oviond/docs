<Update label="2026-10-06" description="Client preview image">

### Choose the image a client shows

Client settings has a new **Preview image** section. It's the picture on the client's card in your Clients list and on shared link previews.

Upload your own from the media library, remove it, or refresh the screenshot of the client's website. The preview in settings is cropped exactly like the card on the Clients list, so what you see is what you get. Refreshing the website screenshot later replaces an uploaded image.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-06" description="Donut chart tooltip fixed">

The donut chart tooltip now appears above the total in the middle instead of behind it.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-05" description="Dashboards can download a PDF again">

### One long page

Every dashboard can export a PDF again. Instead of being cut into sheets, the whole dashboard comes out as a single long page, laid out as it is on screen.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-05" description="Facebook Page Stories">

**Facebook Page Stories** is a new data view, and the video picker now works.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-05" description="PDF page numbers fixed">

Page numbers in a PDF now match the numbers in the editor.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-04" description="New data source: Surnex">

**Surnex** — rank tracking.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-02" description="Meta Ads thumbnails fixed">

Ads with no thumbnail now show a fallback image.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-01" description="Three new data sources">

### Adtraction, Amplitude and ChartMogul

- **Adtraction** — affiliate marketing performance.
- **Amplitude** — product analytics.
- **ChartMogul** — subscription revenue and churn.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-01" description="Instagram and Meta Ads">

- **Instagram media** is split into **Feed**, **Reels** and **Story** views, so each shows the metrics Instagram actually reports for it.
- **Meta Ads custom conversions** can now be used in custom metrics.

</Update>

<Update label="2026-10-01" description="Editor fixes">

- Dashboard widgets can be dropped anywhere on the canvas, not only near the top.
- **Stale widgets** — leaving a report and opening it again could show old widgets.

</Update>
