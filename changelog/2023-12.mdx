<Update label="2023-12-11" description="Instagram retires carousel and story tap metrics">

### Instagram retires carousel and story tap metrics

With Graph API v18, Meta removed the carousel album metrics and the story tap and exit metrics from Instagram media insights. These metrics no longer return data. They are marked **Deprecated** in the widget editor.

- Album Engagement (`carousel_album_engagement`)
- Album Impressions (`carousel_album_impressions`)
- Album Reach (`carousel_album_reach`)
- Album Saved (`carousel_album_saved`)
- Video Views (`carousel_album_video_views`)
- Story Exits (`story_exits`)
- Taps Forward (`story_taps_forward`)
- Taps Back (`story_taps_back`)

</Update>
