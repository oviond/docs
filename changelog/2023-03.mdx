<Update label="2023-03-30" description="Google Business Profile retires old Insights metrics">

### Google Business Profile retires its old Insights metrics

Google turned off the old Business Profile Insights report (`reportInsights` in the My Business v4 API) on March 30, 2023. The Business Profile Performance API replaced it, with different metrics. These metrics no longer return data. They are marked **Deprecated** in the widget editor.

- Direct Queries (`QUERIES_DIRECT`)
- Indirect Queries (`QUERIES_INDIRECT`)
- Chain Queries (`QUERIES_CHAIN`)
- Maps Views (`VIEWS_MAPS`)
- Search Views (`VIEWS_SEARCH`)
- Website Actions (`ACTIONS_WEBSITE`)
- Phone Actions (`ACTIONS_PHONE`)
- Driving Directions Actions (`ACTIONS_DRIVING_DIRECTIONS`)
- Merchant Photos Views (`PHOTOS_VIEWS_MERCHANT`)
- Customers Photos Views (`PHOTOS_VIEWS_CUSTOMERS`)
- Merchant Photos Count (`PHOTOS_COUNT_MERCHANT`)
- Customers Photos Count (`PHOTOS_COUNT_CUSTOMERS`)
- Local Post Search Views (`LOCAL_POST_VIEWS_SEARCH`)
- Local Post Actions - Call To Action (`LOCAL_POST_ACTIONS_CALL_TO_ACTION`)

</Update>
