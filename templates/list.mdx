The **Templates** screen shows every template available to you — those your team saved from its own projects, and the shared **Oviond standard** library that ships with every account.

## Libraries

You filter between libraries from the **Library** filter:

| Filter | What it contains |
|---|---|
| **All** | Both libraries, merged. |
| **Saved by me** | Templates you or your team saved from your own projects. |
| **Oviond standard** | Shared templates maintained by Oviond, usable by every account. |

## Filters and sort

Open the **Filters** popover to narrow the list:

- **Library** — All, Saved by me, or Oviond standard.
- **Type** — All types, Dashboards, or Reports.
- **Data sources** — show only templates whose widgets use specific data sources (e.g. Google Ads).

Use the **search** box (matches template names and data sources) and the **Sort** popover:

- **Recommended** — Oviond's default ranking (shown as **Best match** while you're searching).
- **Newest** — most recently created first.
- **Name A-Z** / **Name Z-A**.

Active filters appear as removable chips above the grid.

## What's on a template card

| Element | Description |
|---|---|
| **Thumbnail** | Auto-generated preview of the template. |
| **Name & description** | Short summary of what the template is for. |
| **Type badge** | **Dashboard** or **Report**. |
| **Data-source icons** | Which data sources the widgets inside expect (up to four, then `+N`). |

Open a template to preview its pages and widgets before using it — see [Apply Template](/templates/apply).

<Note>
Only **Saved by me** templates are selectable and manageable. **Oviond standard** templates can be previewed and used, but not edited or archived.
</Note>
