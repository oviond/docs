---
title: "Delete template"
description: "Archive a template you saved."
---

Archiving a template removes it from the Templates library. Projects you already created from the template **keep** their pages and widgets — archiving the template doesn't touch any project built from it.

Deletes are **soft** — an archived template can be restored from the Archive page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Templates screen">
    From the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the template">
    Use search or the **Filters** popover to locate it. Only **Saved by me** templates can be archived.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the template menu">
    Click the three-dot menu on the template card and choose **Archive**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the **Archive template?** dialog, click **Archive template**. You can restore it later from the Archive page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
To archive several templates at once, select the **Saved by me** templates you want and use the bulk **Archive templates** action.
</Tip>

<Note>
Templates in the **Oviond standard** library can't be archived from your account — they're shared across every customer and maintained by Oviond. Archiving requires permission to manage account features.
</Note>
