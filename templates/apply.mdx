Using a template creates a **brand-new report** from its pages, widgets, and settings, then wires the widgets to the client you choose.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the template">
    From the [Templates](/templates/list) screen, open the template you want. This shows a preview of its pages and widgets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Use template">
    In the preview, click **Use template** to open the slide-over.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a client">
    Pick the **Client** the new report belongs to. You can only create a report for a client you have permission to edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the report">
    Enter a **Name** for the new report. It's pre-filled with the template's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create report">
    Click **Create report**. Oviond copies every page and widget into a new report, applies the client's theme, and opens the report so you can finish wiring its data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens on create

- A new report is created and assigned to the chosen client.
- Pages and widgets are copied from the template.
- The new report inherits the **client's theme** (`theme_id`) when the client has one set.
- Widgets that need a data connection use the new report's client.

<Tip>
Set a **Client theme** in the client's settings before you create reports from templates, and every new report starts on-brand automatically. See [Apply Theme](/themes/apply).
</Tip>
