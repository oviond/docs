---
title: "Assets (saved widgets)"
description: "Save widgets you've built and drop them into any report later."
---

An **asset** is a widget, or a group of widgets, that you saved to reuse. It works like a small template: drop it into any report and it's ready, already set up.

## Save widgets as an asset

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the widgets">
    In the report editor, select one widget, or several with **Multi Select** or Ctrl/⌘ + click.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save as Asset">
    In the floating toolbar, click **Save as Asset**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe it">
    Enter a **Name**, an optional **Description**, and **Tags** to find it later. Turn on **Show in Recommended** to put it near the top of the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. It appears in the **Assets** panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Saving assets needs **Admin access** or **Client access**.

## Use an asset

Open the **Assets** panel in the editor sidebar and drag an asset onto the page. You can also select a widget, click **+**, and pick an asset under **Data Query**.

On a Grid report, the whole asset must fit on the page. If it doesn't, Oviond shows **Page is full**. Click **New Page** to put the asset at the top of a new page after this one. If the asset is taller than a whole page, Oviond tells you it is too tall to add.

## Find assets

In the **Assets** panel:

- **Search assets, tags...** — search by name or tag.
- **Filters** — **Saved by me** or **Oviond standard**, data sources, and tags. **Clear all** removes them.
- **Sort** — change the order.

## Manage assets

On an asset you saved: **Edit** its name, description and tags; **Duplicate** it; or **Archive** it. Archived assets can be restored from **Settings → Archive**.
