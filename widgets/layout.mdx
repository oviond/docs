How widgets move depends on the report's layout, chosen when it was made:

- **Grid** (every dashboard, and Grid reports) — widgets snap to rows and 12 columns, and move up to fill gaps.
- **Free Canvas** reports — place widgets anywhere on the page, at any size.

## Move and resize

- **Move** — drag a widget to a new place.
- **Resize** — drag a corner handle.

## Spacing and measuring (Free Canvas reports)

On a Free Canvas page, red lines show the gaps between the widget you're moving, its neighbours and the page edges. Matching gaps are marked too.

- Use the **arrow keys** to nudge selected widgets by 1 px (hold **Shift** for 10 px).
- Hold **Option** (Mac) or **Alt** (Windows) to turn off snapping and see the distances while you drag, nudge, or just have a widget selected.

On a Free Canvas page, a widget you add with a click (or the **+** menu) and a widget you duplicate go into free space on the page, so they never cover another widget. If the page has no free space left, the widget goes on the next page that has room, or on a new page if none does, and the editor opens that page. Dragging is always free: you can still place a widget anywhere, even over another one.

On a Grid page, a widget you add with a click goes into the first free space that fits it. If there is no room left, Oviond tells you **Page is full**. Click **New Page** to add a page right after this one with the widget on it. The editor opens the new page, so your next widget goes there too.

## Dashboard capacity

A dashboard holds up to 50 widgets. While editing, a counter on the canvas shows how many slots are used — it turns amber as you get close and red once it's full. Reports spread across pages and have no cap.

## Select several widgets

Turn on **Multi Select** in the floating toolbar, or Ctrl/⌘ + click widgets, to select several. With a selection you can [duplicate](/widgets/duplicate) or [delete](/widgets/delete) them in one go, or **Clear Selection**. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/reports/keyboard-shortcuts).

## Saving

Oviond saves as you work. Press Ctrl/⌘ + S to save at once. Every change is kept in [Version history](/reports/version-history).
