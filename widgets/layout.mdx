Widgets use a drag-and-drop grid.

## Move and resize

- **Move** — drag a widget to a new position on the grid.
- **Resize** — drag a corner handle. Widgets snap to the grid.

## Dashboard capacity

A dashboard holds up to 50 widgets. While editing, a counter on the canvas shows how many slots are used — it turns amber as you approach the cap and red once it's reached, so you can rebalance the layout before you run out of room. Reports paginate across pages and have no cap.

## Multi-select

Turn on **Multi Select** in the floating toolbar, then click each widget to add it to the selection. With a selection active you can [duplicate](/widgets/duplicate) or [delete](/widgets/delete) the chosen widgets in one action.

## Saving

Oviond saves the whole project atomically — **every page and every widget** is persisted in one operation — and also auto-saves as you work.
