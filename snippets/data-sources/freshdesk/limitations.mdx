**Limits set by Freshdesk**

- **Requests per minute by plan.** Freshdesk limits API calls per minute for the whole account: 50 on trials, 100 on Growth, 400 on Pro, 700 on Enterprise. Many Freshdesk widgets can show **Freshdesk is busy right now**.
- **Search stops at 300 tickets.** Freshdesk's ticket search returns at most 10 pages of 30 tickets.

Freshdesk's pages: [API docs (rate limits, search)](https://developers.freshdesk.com/api/).
