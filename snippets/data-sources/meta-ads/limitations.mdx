**Limits set by Meta**

- **History is 37 months.** Meta keeps ad totals for about 3 years (37 months). Older dates show no data.
- **Some splits only go back 13 months.** Since January 12, 2026, numbers of unique people (like unique clicks) split by something such as age go back 13 months only. Numbers split by hour also go back 13 months. Numbers split by frequency go back 6 months. Totals that are not split still go back 37 months.
- **7 Day View and 28 Day View return nothing.** Since January 12, 2026, Meta no longer gives conversions for these two windows. They are still in the **Attribution Window** list under **Show Advanced Settings**, but they now count 0. A widget that uses only them shows no conversions. If it also uses other windows, only those count. Use **1 Day View**, **1 Day Click**, **7 Day Click** or **28 Day Click** instead, or leave the list empty (**All Attribution Windows**).
- **Recent conversions can still change.** A conversion can arrive days after the ad was seen or clicked. Numbers for the last few days can go up.
- **Website and app conversions don't split by Region.** Meta doesn't give conversions that happen off Facebook and Instagram (on your website or in your app) when the widget is split by **Region**. Clicks, impressions and spend still split by region.
- **Some splits can't show which one a website conversion belongs to.** With **Action Device**, **Action Destination**, **Action target ID**, **Product ID**, **Carousel Card ID**, **Carousel Card Name** or **Canvas Component Name**, website conversions still count, but their row has no device, card or product. App conversions aren't given at all.
- **Big widgets can time out.** Meta stops a request that asks for too much at once. A widget with a long date range and many splits can show **Meta Ads isn't responding**. Use a shorter date range or fewer metrics and dimensions.
- **Days follow the ad account's time zone.** Meta dates every number in the ad account's own time zone. So a day can differ from a day in another tool.
