**Limits set by Meta**

- **Numbers can be 2 days late.** Meta says the data behind the numbers can be up to 48 hours late. The last 2 days can still go up.
- **History is 2 years.** Meta keeps post numbers for up to 2 years.
- **Stories last 24 hours.** Meta only gives stories, and their numbers, while the story is live (24 hours). So **Media Story** only shows stories posted in the last 24 hours. Live video stories are never listed. A story seen by fewer than 5 people has no numbers.
- **Album posts have no insights.** Meta gives no numbers for the photos and videos inside an album (carousel).
- **Likes, comments and views on posts are free (organic) only.** Interactions that came through ads are not counted in them.
- **Small accounts get fewer numbers.** **New Followers** needs 100 or more followers. Follower splits (age, gender, city, country) need 100 or more followers. Engaged audience splits need 100 or more engagements.
- **Splits show the top 45.** Age, gender, city and country splits only list the top 45 values. People with no known age, gender or place are left out, so the split can add up to less than your followers.
- **Hashtags: 30 a week.** An Instagram account can look up at most 30 different hashtags in 7 days. Each hashtag counts for 7 days from its first lookup. Hashtags in stories and hashtags with emojis are not supported. Meta gives an error for hashtags it finds sensitive.
- **Recent hashtag posts are from the last 24 hours.** Meta's recent posts for a hashtag only cover the last 24 hours.
