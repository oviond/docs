**Limits set by BigCommerce**

- **Requests per 30 seconds.** BigCommerce limits API requests by store plan: Growth and Core stores get 150 every 30 seconds (20,000 an hour), Scale stores 450 every 30 seconds. All apps on a store share this. A big store, other apps, or many BigCommerce widgets can show **BigCommerce is busy right now**.

BigCommerce's pages: [API rate limits](https://developer.bigcommerce.com/docs/start/best-practices/api-rate-limits).
