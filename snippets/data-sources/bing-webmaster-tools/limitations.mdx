**Limits set by Bing**

- **Queries and Pages update once a week.** Bing updates its top queries and top pages data every week, so the newest days can be missing. **Traffic** updates every day.
- **Top queries and top pages only.** Bing gives its top queries and top pages, not every one.

Bing's pages: [query stats](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/microsoft.bing.webmaster.api.interfaces.iwebmasterapi.getquerystats), [page stats](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/microsoft.bing.webmaster.api.interfaces.iwebmasterapi.getpagestats), [rank and traffic stats](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/microsoft.bing.webmaster.api.interfaces.iwebmasterapi.getrankandtrafficstats).
