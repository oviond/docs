**Limits set by Pinterest**

- **Only the last 90 days.** Pinterest's account analytics only go back 90 days from today, and one request covers at most 90 days. An **Account** widget whose dates start earlier, or cover more than 90 days, gets an error from Pinterest and shows **These settings aren't supported by Pinterest**. With a comparison, the earlier period must also be inside the last 90 days.
- **Days are in UTC.** Pinterest reports days in UTC.

Pinterest's pages: [account analytics](https://developers.pinterest.com/docs/api/v5/user_account-analytics).
