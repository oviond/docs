**Limits set by Harvest**

- **Reports have a low limit.** Harvest allows 100 report requests every 15 minutes (other requests: 100 every 15 seconds). Each **Project Budget** widget uses report requests when it loads, so several of them can show **Harvest is busy right now** for a while.
- **Only projects with a budget.** Harvest's budget report only lists projects that have a budget set.
- **What you see depends on your Harvest role.** The connected Harvest user only sees what their role allows. Money budgets (budget, spent and remaining) are only shown to Administrators, and to Project Managers with the "View billable rates and amounts" permission.

Harvest's pages: [rate limits](https://help.getharvest.com/api-v2/introduction/overview/general/), [project budget report](https://help.getharvest.com/api-v2/reports-api/reports/project-budget-report/).
