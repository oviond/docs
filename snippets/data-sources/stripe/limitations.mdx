**Limits set by Stripe**

- **25 requests a second per list.** Stripe allows 100 API requests a second in live mode, and 25 a second for each kind of list. Big accounts with many Stripe widgets can show **Stripe is busy right now**.
- **A monthly read budget.** Stripe expects reads to stay under an average of 500 per transaction over 30 days, with at least 10,000 reads a month allowed. A small account with big widgets that refresh often can reach this.

Stripe's pages: [rate limits](https://docs.stripe.com/rate-limits).
