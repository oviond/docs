**Limits set by HubSpot**

- **About 110 requests every 10 seconds.** HubSpot limits how often an app signed in with OAuth, like Oviond, can call each HubSpot account (110 requests every 10 seconds for marketplace apps). Many HubSpot widgets loading at once can show **HubSpot is busy right now**.
- **Your HubSpot plan decides what Performance shows.** **Performance** reads HubSpot's website analytics reports, and HubSpot gives this data based on the account's subscription.

HubSpot's pages: [API usage limits](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/developer-tooling/platform/usage-guidelines), [analytics reports (legacy)](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/legacy/reporting-v2/get-analytics-v2-reports-breakdown_by-time_period).
