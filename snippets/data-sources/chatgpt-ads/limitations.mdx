**Limits set by OpenAI**

- **History is 365 days.** ChatGPT Ads keeps day-by-day and total delivery numbers for the most recent 365 days. Older dates show no data.
- **Recent numbers can still change.** Impressions, clicks and CTR can show within minutes but may change as processing finishes. Spend, CPC and CPM can update later. Conversions update once a day, so allow at least a day, and recent ones can still change.
- **Today and this month are partial.** Reports for the current day or month only hold what has happened so far.
- **Money and days follow the ad account.** Spend is in the ad account's currency, and days follow the ad account's time zone.
- **Request limits.** OpenAI allows 600 requests a minute per endpoint and 1,200 a minute in total, per ad account and per IP address.

OpenAI's pages: [reporting and data freshness](https://developers.openai.com/ads/reporting), [API overview](https://developers.openai.com/ads/api-overview).
