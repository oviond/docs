**Limits set by Appfigures**

- **100 requests a day on free and trial plans.** Appfigures allows 100 API requests a day on free and trial plans and 1,000 on paid plans. The day runs from midnight to midnight UTC. One widget can use up to 10 requests. Past the limit, widgets show **We couldn't load this data** until the next day.

Appfigures' pages: [rate limits](https://docs.appfigures.com/rate-limits/), [keyword ranks](https://docs.appfigures.com/reference/aso-keyword-ranks/).
