**Limits set by Pinterest**

- **Only the last 90 days.** Pinterest's ad analytics only go back 90 days from today, and one request covers at most 90 days. A widget whose dates start earlier, or cover more than 90 days, gets an error from Pinterest and shows **These settings aren't supported by Pinterest Ads**. With a comparison, the earlier period must also be inside the last 90 days.
- **Days are in UTC.** Pinterest reports days in UTC unless asked otherwise, so a day can differ from your ad account's time zone.
- **Standard conversion windows.** Pinterest Tag conversions count clicks up to 30 days before, and views up to 1 day before.

Pinterest's pages: [ad account analytics](https://developers.pinterest.com/docs/api/v5/ad_account-analytics).
