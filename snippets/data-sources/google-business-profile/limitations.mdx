**Limits set by Google**

- **Only verified profiles have data.** Google only gives performance numbers for verified Business Profiles. An unverified location shows no data.
- **Search keywords come by the month.** Google counts search keywords per whole month. Google adds each month's keywords at the start of the next month, and it can take up to 5 days. The current month has no keywords yet.
- **Rare keywords have no exact number.** For keywords used by few people, Google only says the count is below a small number, not the count itself.
- **Views count people, not visits.** Google counts a person at most once a day, and only a few times across devices and apps (Search, Maps). So views can be lower than the numbers in Google's emails.
- **Ads count too.** Views, searches and actions include people who came from Google Ads, not only free search results.
- **Direction requests are cleaned up.** Google leaves out repeated taps, cancelled requests and spam, so directions can be lower than the taps people made.

Google's pages: [Business Profile performance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9918094), [search keywords API](https://developers.google.com/my-business/reference/performance/rest/v1/locations.searchkeywords.impressions.monthly/list).
