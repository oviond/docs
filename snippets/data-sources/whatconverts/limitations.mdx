**Limits set by WhatConverts**

- **Up to 400 days.** WhatConverts lets one request cover at most 400 days. A widget whose dates are longer gets an error from WhatConverts.
- **Days are in UTC.** WhatConverts reads the dates in UTC, so a day can differ from your own time zone.

WhatConverts' pages: [leads API](https://www.whatconverts.com/api/leads/).
