**Limits set by Snapchat**

- **Numbers become final after 48 hours.** Snapchat updates stats about every 15 minutes, but they are only final 48 hours after the end of each day. Recent days can still change.
- **Some metrics allow only 31 days.** For some metrics (like installs), Snapchat only gives day-by-day numbers for up to 31 days per request. A longer widget with these metrics can show an error. Big requests can also be turned away as too large, and the widget shows **These settings aren't supported by Snapchat Ads**; use fewer metrics or a shorter range.
- **Days follow the ad account's time zone.** Snapchat reports each day in the ad account's own time zone.
- **Standard conversion settings.** By default, Snapchat dates conversions by the day they happened, and counts swipe-ups up to 28 days before and views up to 1 day before.

Snapchat's pages: [measurement API](https://developers.snap.com/marketing-api/Ads-API/measurement).
