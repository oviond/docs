**Limits set by LinkedIn**

- **History.** LinkedIn keeps ad performance data for 10 years. Splits by people's job details (job title, job function, seniority, industry, company, company size, country and region) only go back 2 years.
- **Job-detail splits come 1 to 2 days late.** Numbers split by job details can be 24 to 48 hours late. Video watch time can be up to 48 hours late. Other numbers are near real time.
- **Job-detail splits are close, not exact.** To protect members' privacy, LinkedIn gives approximate numbers for these splits. Values with fewer than 3 events are left out, and ads with too few people engaging show no split at all. So the split can add up to less than the total.
- **Only the top 100 job details per ad per day.** For example, if an ad was seen by people with 1,000 job titles, LinkedIn only gives the 100 titles with the most impressions for each day.
- **Company splits can show companies you didn't target.** LinkedIn can list companies that weren't in your targeting.
- **Some metrics don't work with job-detail splits.** LinkedIn doesn't give every metric for these splits.
