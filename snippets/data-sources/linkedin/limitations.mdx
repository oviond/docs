**Limits set by LinkedIn**

- **History is 12 months.** LinkedIn only gives post and follower numbers by date for the last 12 months. Older dates show no data.
- **The last 2 days are missing.** Follower numbers by date stop 2 days before today.
- **Free (organic) activity only.** Post numbers don't count sponsored (paid) activity. Paid numbers are in **LinkedIn Ads**.
- **Splits show the top 100.** Follower splits (country, industry, job function and so on) list only the top 100 values.
- **Reposts count differently.** **Page Shares** by date don't count instant reposts, so they can be lower than the all-time number.

LinkedIn's pages: [post statistics](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/community-management/organizations/share-statistics), [follower statistics](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/community-management/organizations/follower-statistics), [page statistics](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/community-management/organizations/page-statistics).
