**Limits set by Google**

- **Numbers are 2 to 3 days late.** YouTube Analytics data takes 48 to 72 hours to process, so the last days of a date range are empty. YouTube Studio's live counts can be higher.
- **Small numbers are hidden.** To protect viewers' privacy, Google leaves out rows with too few views. This affects age and gender, countries and regions, and traffic details like search terms and outside websites. So a split can add up to less than the total. Google doesn't say where the line is.
- **Deleted videos can be missing.** Reports that list videos one by one can leave out deleted videos.

Google's pages: [YouTube Analytics data](https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/data_model), [channel reports](https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/channel_reports).
