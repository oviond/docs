**Limits set by Meta**

- **Impressions and likes were removed.** On November 15, 2025, Meta removed **Total Impressions** and **Page Likes** from Page insights. A widget that uses one of them shows **These settings aren't supported by Facebook**, even for its other metrics. Edit the widget and pick the new metric. The old metrics are marked **Deprecated** in the **Metrics & dimensions** tab.
- **The new metrics.** Impressions became **Page Media View** (views). Likes became **Page Follows** and **Total Followers**.
- **Viral and non-viral impressions have no new version.** Meta removed them for Pages and posts and named nothing to use instead.
- **Small Pages get no insights.** Meta only gives Page insights for Pages with 100 or more likes. A smaller Page shows no data.
- **History is 2 years.** Meta keeps Page insights for the last 2 years. Older dates show no data.
- **Most numbers update once a day.** Most metrics update once every 24 hours, so today's numbers are often empty or low.
- **Age, gender and place need 100 people.** Splits by age, gender, city or country only show when there is data for 100 or more people.
- **Reach doesn't always add up.** Meta counts unique people separately for each number. So total reach may not equal paid reach plus organic reach.
- **Paid numbers of boosted posts include organic.** When you boost a post, its paid impressions also include its organic reach.
- **Some video numbers are only right for your own videos.** Some video metrics are only right for the Page that made the video. A video shared from another Page shows 0.
- **Reel interactions are not counted.** Interactions on Reels are not in Page insights.

Meta's pages: [Page Insights changes](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2025/08/15/page-insights-api-updates/), [removed metrics and what to use instead](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/pages-api/platforminsights/page/deprecated-metrics), [Page Insights limits](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/reference/v24.0/insights#Reading).
