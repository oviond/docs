**Limits set by Klaviyo**

- **Reports are limited to 2 a minute and 225 a day.** Klaviyo lets apps request campaign and flow reports only 1 time a second in short bursts, 2 times a minute and 225 times a day.
- **Campaign numbers keep growing after you send.** People open, click and buy for days after a campaign goes out, so recent campaigns' numbers can still go up.

Klaviyo's pages: [campaign values report (rate limits)](https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_campaign_values), [rate limits](https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/docs/rate_limits_and_error_handling).
