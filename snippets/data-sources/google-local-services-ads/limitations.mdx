**Limits set by Google**

- **Daily data goes back 37 months.** Since June 1, 2026, Google keeps day-by-day ad numbers for 37 months only. A **Campaigns** widget whose dates start more than 37 months ago can show **These settings aren't supported by Google Local Services Ads** instead of numbers.
- **Some leads have no contact details.** When Google wipes a lead's data (lead status `WIPED_OUT`), it sends no contact details, so that lead shows no name, phone or email.
- **Healthcare leads are not available.** Google doesn't give leads in healthcare categories, so they never appear in **Leads**.

Google's pages: [Local Services campaigns](https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/docs/campaigns/local-service-campaigns), [data retention](https://developers.google.com/ads/blog/posts/2026/05/new-data-retention-policy-for-google).
