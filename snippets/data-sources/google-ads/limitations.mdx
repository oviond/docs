**Limits set by Google**

- **Daily data goes back 37 months.** Since June 1, 2026, Google keeps day-by-day numbers for 37 months only. Google answers with an error when asked for older days, so a widget whose dates start more than 37 months ago can show **These settings aren't supported by Google Ads** instead of numbers.
- **Recent numbers can still change.** Clicks, impressions and cost usually update within about an hour. Conversions can take longer. Impression share updates once a day, and search click share about 3 days late.
- **Conversions are dated by the ad click, not the sale.** A sale today from a click last week counts on last week's date. So recent days can show fewer conversions now, and more later. Google Analytics 4 dates it by the sale, so its numbers can differ.
- **Some search terms are hidden.** Google leaves out search terms that too few people searched, to protect privacy. Their clicks still count in the campaign totals, so the **Search Terms** view can add up to less than the campaign.
- **Performance Max has no ad groups or keywords.** It uses asset groups instead. Its campaigns show in the **Campaigns** and **Asset Groups** views, but not in **Ad Groups**, **Ads**, **Search Keywords**, **Search Terms**, **Age**, **Gender**, **Household Income** or **Parental Status**.
- **Placement is mostly the Display Network.** The **Placement** view lists the websites, apps and videos where ads showed, mainly on the Google Display Network. It mostly leaves out other networks, like Search.
- **Locations and Geographic are not the same.** **Locations** shows only the places your campaigns target. **Geographic** shows where people were, or what place they showed interest in, so its places can differ from your targets.
