---
title: "Permanently delete"
description: "Remove archived items from your account forever."
---

Permanently deleting an archived item is **irreversible** — it's deleted for good, with everything in it (a client's reports and widgets, a report's pages and widgets, a template's pages and widgets, and so on). For media, the file itself is deleted too.

Use it to:

- Free up plan quota immediately
- Remove sensitive data after a client requests erasure
- Clean up test data with certainty

<Warning>
There is no undo. Once an item is permanently deleted, it cannot be recovered — not even by Oviond support.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Archive">
    Go to **Settings → Archive** (`/settings/archive`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter and select">
    Narrow to the type you want to remove, then check each item. Multi-select works for bulk delete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Delete">
    A confirmation dialog warns that the items will be permanently deleted. Confirm to proceed, or choose **Keep archived** to cancel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The Archive needs **Admin access**. Other members can't permanently delete items.
</Note>
