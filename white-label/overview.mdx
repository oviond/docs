title: "White-Label Reporting for Your Agency"

Oviond's white-label features let you replace every visible trace of Oviond branding with your own. When a client opens a shared report link, they see your agency name, your logo, and — if you've set one up — your own domain. Oviond stays invisible.

## What white labeling covers

White-label settings apply to the experience your clients see when they view a shared report. This includes:

- **Agency name and tagline** — displayed in the client-facing report header and on shared links.
- **Logo** — your agency logo replaces Oviond's in the report frame. You can upload separate versions for light and dark backgrounds.
- **Color accents** — the header and interface chrome in shared report views reflect your brand colors.
- **Custom domain** — reports are served from your domain (for example, `reports.youragency.com`) instead of `oviond.com`. See [Set Up a Custom Domain](/white-label/custom-domains).
- **Email sender name** — the name that appears in scheduled report delivery emails sent to your clients.

Your clients never see a reference to Oviond — only your agency brand.

## Navigate to white-label settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your account settings">
    Click your avatar or initials in the bottom-left corner of the sidebar, then select **Account Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the White Label tab">
    In the account settings panel, click the **White Label** tab. This is where all agency branding options live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set your agency name and tagline

Your agency name appears in the client-facing report header and in any email notifications sent to clients. The tagline, if set, appears beneath the name as a short descriptor.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open White Label settings">
    Go to **Account Settings** > **White Label**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your agency name">
    In the **Agency name** field, type your agency's name exactly as you want clients to see it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a tagline (optional)">
    In the **Tagline** field, enter a short phrase — for example, "Digital marketing intelligence" or "Your performance partner." Keep it brief.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save**. The updated name and tagline are applied to all client-facing report views immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Upload your agency logo

You can upload two versions of your logo: one for light backgrounds and one for dark backgrounds. Oviond automatically shows the correct version depending on the report's color theme.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open White Label settings">
    Go to **Account Settings** > **White Label**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the light logo">
    Under **Logo (light background)**, click **Upload** and select your logo file. Use a PNG or SVG with a transparent background for best results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the dark logo">
    Under **Logo (dark background)**, click **Upload** and select a version of your logo suited for dark backgrounds — typically a white or light-colored variant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview the result">
    Use the inline preview to confirm both logos look correct before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save**. Your logos are now applied to all shared report views.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you only upload one logo version, Oviond uses it for both light and dark contexts. Upload both versions to ensure your logo looks correct regardless of the report theme.
</Note>

## How white-label settings apply

White-label settings are account-wide. Once configured, they apply automatically to:

- **All shared report links** — any public link you generate in Oviond shows your branding, not Oviond's.
- **Client-facing report views** — the header, navigation frame, and report chrome all reflect your agency brand.
- **Scheduled report emails** — delivery emails sent to clients use your agency name as the sender name.

You don't need to configure white labeling per report. Set it once and it applies everywhere.

## White-label settings vs. per-report branding

White-label settings and branding settings serve different purposes:

| Setting | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **White-label settings** | Your agency identity in the report frame — name, logo, domain, email sender. |
| **Branding settings** | The visual design inside client-facing reports — color palette, fonts, report-level logo. |

Use white-label settings to establish your agency's presence. Use branding settings to control how report content looks to clients. See [Customize Report Branding and Appearance](/white-label/branding) for details on branding settings.
