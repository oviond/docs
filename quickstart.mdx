Oviond is a white-label marketing reporting platform. This quickstart walks you through signing up, creating a client, connecting a data source, and building your first report so you can see live client data in minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [app.oviond.com](https://app.oviond.com) and click **Sign up**. Click **Continue with Google**, or enter your email — there's no password to set at sign-up, and Oviond emails you a confirmation link to click. See [Sign up and sign in](/account/sign-in).

    <Note>
      If you're joining an existing account, ask your admin to send you an invitation instead of signing up directly.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete onboarding">
    On your first sign-in, Oviond asks for **Your name**, **Company name** and **Company website** on one screen. It then makes your company your first client, with a starter dashboard, and opens the **Clients** page. See [Onboarding](/onboarding/overview).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a client">
    On the **Clients** page, click **Add Client**. Enter the required **Client name** and **Website**. Under **Setup details** you can also set the contact's first name, manager, currency, timezone, folder, report domain and theme. A client groups all of its data sources, dashboards and reports. Every report belongs to a client.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a report">
    Inside a client, go to **Reports** and click **Add Report**. Choose to start from scratch or from a template. If you start from scratch, pick a type: a **Dashboard** (a live view that always shows the latest data) or a **Multi-Page Report** (a paged report for a specific date range). Then name your report and pick a theme.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect a data source">
    With the report open, go to the **Data sources** tab in the editor sidebar. You can also connect from the client's own [Data sources](/data-sources/client-sources) page, without opening a report. Search for a platform (for example, Google Ads or Meta Ads) and select it to start the connection wizard. Connect with OAuth or an API key, depending on the platform.

    You can review and manage connected data sources later under **Settings → Data sources**. See [Connecting Data Sources](/data-sources/connect) for the full list of supported platforms and connection steps.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add widgets">
    In the editor, drag widgets onto the page from the **Add Content** panel. Each widget is tied to a metric and the data source you connected.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Share with your client">
    Click **Share** in the top toolbar to open the share dialog. Use the **Link** tab to copy a public link your client can view without logging in — you can toggle the link active, require a password, or allow PDF download. Use the **Email** tab to send the report by email, or the **Download** tab to export a PDF.

    To send reports on a recurring schedule, set up an [automation](/automations/scheduled) instead. To apply your branding, see [White Label Overview](/custom-domains/list).
  </Step>
</Steps>
