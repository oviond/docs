---
title: "Add Agency Report"
description: "Create an account-level project not tied to any specific client."
---

An **Agency Report** is a project that lives at the account level — no `client_id`. Use it for internal dashboards, pitch decks, or cross-client rollups.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Agency">
    Click **Agency** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click + New Agency Report">
    Opens the create dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the report">
    Shown on the shared link and in any PDF exports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the type">
    **Report** (paged) or **Dashboard** (single-scroll).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start blank or from a template">
    Templates work the same way they do for client projects — see [Add Template](/templates/add).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Oviond opens the editor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Data sources in agency reports

Agency reports pull from the agency's **shared integrations** — data sources connected at the account level rather than to a specific client. Connect them from **Data Sources** and they become available to every agency report.
