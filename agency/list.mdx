---
title: "List Agency Reports"
description: "Browse every account-level report."
---

The **Agency** screen lists every agency report in your account. These aren't scoped to a client, so they don't appear under any client's workspace.

## What's on the card

| Element | Description |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Click to open the editor. |
| **Type** | Report or Dashboard. |
| **Last edited** | Timestamp of the most recent save. |

## Quick actions

From any agency-report card's three-dot menu:

- **Open** — enter the editor.
- **Duplicate** — see [Duplicate Project](/projects/duplicate) (agency reports use the same duplicate flow).
- **Share** — see [Share Project](/projects/share).
- **Archive** — soft-delete to the [Archive](/archive/view).

<Note>
Agency reports use the same pages/widgets, date-range, and share features as client projects. The only difference is scope — see [Add Agency Report](/agency/add) for details on when to use one.
</Note>
