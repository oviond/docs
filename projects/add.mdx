---
title: "Add Project"
description: "Create a new report or dashboard under any client."
---

A **project** is a named report or dashboard assigned to a single client. Every project holds its own pages and widgets and can be shared publicly via a link.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Reports screen">
    Click **Reports** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click + New Project">
    Opens the new-project dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your project">
    The name appears on the shared link, in PDF exports, and in the email subject when the project is automated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the type">
    Choose **Report** (static paged report) or **Dashboard** (single-scroll live view).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a client">
    Use the **Client** dropdown. If the client doesn't exist yet, see [Add Client](/clients/add) first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start blank or from a template">
    Starting from a [template](/templates/list) pre-populates pages and widgets you can customize. Otherwise start blank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create**. Oviond opens the project editor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Need an account-level project that isn't tied to a single client? See [Add Agency Report](/agency/add).
</Tip>
