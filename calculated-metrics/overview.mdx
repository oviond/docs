---
title: "Calculated metrics"
description: "Build custom KPIs from formulas that combine metrics across your data sources."
---

A **calculated metric** is a custom KPI defined by a formula — for example `Revenue / Sessions` or `(Clicks / Impressions) * 100`. You combine metrics from your connected data sources with operators and number constants, then use the result in widgets like any other metric.

Calculated metrics are managed inside the report editor, on the **Calculated Metrics** tab.

## Use a calculated metric

Add a **Calculated Metric** widget from **Add Content** (or from a widget's **+** menu), then pick your metric under **Metrics**. You can chart it, show it as a single number, or add it to a table, like any other metric.

## Find calculated metrics

On the **Calculated Metrics** tab:

- **Search metrics...** — search by name.
- **Filter** — show only metrics whose formula uses the data sources you pick. **Clear all** removes them.
- **Sort** — **Newest**, **Oldest**, **Name A-Z** or **Name Z-A**.

Your search, filter and sort stay while the report is open.
