---
title: "Archive a calculated metric"
description: "Archive a calculated metric you no longer need. You can restore it later."
---

Archiving a calculated metric moves it to the [Archive](/archive/view). You can restore it from there at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Calculated Metrics tab">
    In the report editor, open the **Calculated Metrics** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archive the metric">
    On the metric's row, click **Archive**, then confirm in the **Archive Calculated Metric** dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Widgets that use the archived metric stop showing data until you restore it or point them at another metric.
</Warning>

To bring it back, go to **Settings → Archive**, find the metric and click **Restore**.

## API

`DELETE /v1/calculated-metrics/{id}` archives a calculated metric.
