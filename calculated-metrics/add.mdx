---
title: "Add a calculated metric"
description: "Build a custom KPI from a formula and set how it displays."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the report">
    From the client's **Reports** tab, open the report or dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Calculated Metrics tab">
    In the editor sidebar, open the **Calculated Metrics** tab and click **Add Calculated Metric**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the formula">
    Under **Formula**, add parts one by one:

    - **Add New Metric** — a metric from a connected data source (pick the **Data Source**, the metric and, if you want, a dimension).
    - **Add Operator** — `+`, `-`, `×`, `÷`, and brackets to set the order.
    - **Set Fixed Value** — a number.

    The editor tells you **Valid formula** or **Invalid formula**. Click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and format">
    Under **Details**, enter a **Name** and choose the **Format**: **Numeric**, **Decimal**, **Percentage**, or **Currency**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The metric is now available in every report in your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Each metric token is a normal data-source query, so a single formula can combine metrics from different platforms — for example ad spend from one source divided by conversions from another.
</Tip>

## API

`POST /v1/calculated-metrics` creates a calculated metric.
