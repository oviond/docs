## Sign up

Go to [app.oviond.com](https://app.oviond.com) and choose **Sign up** (*Start your free trial* — no credit card required). Either:

- click **Continue with Google**, or
- enter your work email and click **Continue**. Oviond emails you a confirmation link; click it to finish.

You don't set a password at sign-up. You can add one later in [My account](/account/password). Then Oviond opens a short [setup screen](/onboarding/overview).

<Note>
Joining a team? Ask an admin to [invite you](/users/invite) instead of signing up — otherwise you'd get a separate account.
</Note>

## Sign in

Enter your email on **Sign in to your Oviond account**, then choose how:

- **Continue with Google** — if your account uses that Google email.
- **Continue with Password** — enter your password.
- **Continue with Magic Link** — Oviond emails you a link that signs you in. Use this if you never set a password.

## Forgot your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for a reset link">
    On the sign-in screen, click **Forgot your password?**, enter your email and click **Send reset link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the email">
    Click the link in the email. A link that's old or already used says *This reset link is no longer valid* — click **Request a new reset link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a new password">
    Enter a **New password**, confirm it, and click **Reset Password**. You can sign in with it straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Accept an invite

Click the link in your invite email. Oviond sets up your account (*Setting up your account...*) and signs you in. If the link has expired, ask an admin to **Resend invite** — see [Invite a user](/users/invite).
