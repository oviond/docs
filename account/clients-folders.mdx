title: "Organize Clients and Folders in Oviond"
description: "Learn how to create client workspaces, organize them into folders, and manage your full client list in Oviond for a cleaner agency workflow."
---

Oviond uses a two-level structure to keep your agency's work organized: **clients** are the top-level containers that hold all the reports, dashboards, and integrations for a given client account, and **folders** let you group multiple clients together for easier navigation.

As your client list grows, folders help you segment by industry, team, region, or any other grouping that fits your agency's workflow.

## Clients

### What is a client?

A client in Oviond is a dedicated workspace for one of your agency's clients. Everything related to that client — their connected data sources (integrations), reports, and dashboards — lives inside their client workspace. Clients are listed on your main dashboard and are accessible from the left sidebar.

<Note>
  The number of clients you can create is determined by your plan. You can check how many you've used under **Account Settings → Usage**. See [Plans and billing](/billing/plans) for limit details.
</Note>

### Create a client

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your dashboard">
    From the main Oviond interface, navigate to your client list (the home dashboard).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add client">
    Click **Add client** or the **+** button to open the new client dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the client's name">
    Type the client's name. This name appears throughout Oviond and, depending on your white-label setup, may appear in client-facing reports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add optional details">
    You can optionally add a website URL, industry, or other identifying details to help your team distinguish clients at a glance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the client">
    Click **Create client** (or **Save**). The new client workspace is created and added to your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Bulk delete clients

If you need to clean up your workspace, you can delete multiple clients at once. Deleting a client permanently removes the client workspace and all its reports, dashboards, and integration connections.

<Warning>
  Bulk deleting clients is irreversible. All reports, dashboards, and integration connections associated with the selected clients will be permanently deleted. Export any data or reports you need before proceeding.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your client list">
    Navigate to your main dashboard where your clients are listed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable multi-select">
    Click the checkbox that appears when you hover over a client card, or look for a **Select** mode toggle. Select all the clients you want to delete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    With clients selected, click **Delete** from the action bar that appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the deletion">
    Review the list of clients to be deleted in the confirmation dialog, then click **Confirm delete**. The selected client workspaces are permanently removed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Folders

### What is a folder?

A folder is a way to group multiple clients together under a shared label. Folders appear in your sidebar and on your dashboard, letting you quickly navigate to a subset of clients without scrolling through your full list.

For example, you might create folders like **E-commerce Clients**, **Healthcare**, or **US Region** to reflect how your team is organized.

<Note>
  Folders are purely organizational — they don't affect permissions, billing, or how reports are built. A client can only belong to one folder at a time.
</Note>

### Create a folder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the folder management area">
    On your main dashboard or in the sidebar, look for a **New folder** or **Add folder** button. This is often accessible from the **Folders** section of the sidebar or via a menu on your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the folder">
    Enter a name for your folder (for example, **Retail Clients** or **North America**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the folder">
    Click **Create** or **Save**. The folder now appears in your sidebar and is ready to have clients assigned to it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Assign clients to a folder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the client you want to assign">
    Find the client on your dashboard or in the client list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the client's options">
    Click the three-dot menu on the client card and select **Move to folder** (or **Assign to folder**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the target folder">
    Choose the folder from the list of available folders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Move** or **Save**. The client now appears inside the selected folder. It will no longer be visible in your unorganized client list unless you navigate to **All clients**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Rename a folder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the folder">
    Locate the folder in your sidebar or on your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open folder options">
    Click the three-dot menu next to the folder name and select **Rename**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the new name">
    Type the new folder name and press **Enter** or click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Delete a folder

Deleting a folder removes the folder grouping but does not delete the clients inside it. Any clients that were in the deleted folder move back to your main unorganized client list.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the folder">
    Locate the folder in your sidebar or dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open folder options">
    Click the three-dot menu next to the folder name and select **Delete**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm deletion">
    Confirm the action. The folder is removed and its clients are returned to your main client list.
  </Step>
</Steps>
