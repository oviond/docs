---
title: "Delete account"
description: "Permanently delete your Oviond account and all its data."
---

Deleting your account is **permanent and irreversible**. It removes the entire workspace — every client, report, page, widget, data source, branding asset, and media file — and removes every team member from the account.

<Warning>
  A deleted account doesn't go to the Archive and can't be restored. Every team member loses access the moment the account is deleted. Export anything you need to keep before you proceed.
</Warning>

## Before you delete

- **Cancel your subscription first.** While a subscription is active, **Delete account** is turned off and the page says *You have an active subscription. Cancel it before deleting your account.* Click **Go to billing** and cancel it in the billing portal. See [Subscription](/billing/subscription).
- **Export critical reports** — save a PDF of every report you want to keep.
- **Tell your team** — every user in the account loses access immediately.

## How to delete

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open My account">
    Go to **Settings → My account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to the Danger zone">
    At the bottom of the page, find the **Danger zone** and click **Delete account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with your email">
    In the confirmation dialog, type your account email address exactly (**Type your email to confirm**) to turn on the button. Choose **Keep account** to cancel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm deletion">
    Confirm to delete the account. Oviond removes the account and all its data, then signs you out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Deleting the account requires **Admin access**. The **Delete account** button appears for everyone, but only admins can complete the deletion — other users receive an error if they try.
</Note>
