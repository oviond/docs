---
title: "PDF export"
description: "Make a downloadable PDF of a report or dashboard."
---

Oviond makes a PDF of a **Multi-Page Report** or a **Dashboard** in the background, so a big report doesn't slow your browser down.

<Note>
A Multi-Page Report prints page by page. A Dashboard has no pages, so it prints as one long page the size of the whole dashboard — nothing is split.
</Note>

## The PDF shows the saved data

The PDF prints the numbers saved in the report at that moment. It doesn't fetch new data while it prints. This makes PDFs faster, and they match what you see in the report.

So if the numbers look old, refresh the report first, then make the PDF. The Download tab reminds you:

> The PDF uses the data saved in the report right now. If numbers look old, refresh the report first.

On a shared link, viewers see: *The PDF shows the report as it was last saved. It may not match the dates or numbers you see here.*

## Make a PDF

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the share dialog">
    From the report, open **Share** and switch to the **Download** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate New PDF">
    The button shows **Generating** (or how many are **Queued**) while it works. A new row appears in the list, marked **Processing...**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    When it's ready, click **Download** on the row. The PDF opens in a new tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove a PDF from the list, click **Archive** on its row and confirm in the **Archive PDF** dialog. It moves to the [Archive](/archive/view).

A PDF that isn't ready after 30 minutes is marked as failed. Make a new one.

## Over the API

Making a PDF takes two calls:

1. `POST /v1/pdf/generate` queues the job and returns a `job_id`. Send the `report_id`, `client_id`, the page `format` (**A4** or **Letter**) and the `orientation` (**portrait** or **landscape**). The defaults are `A4` and `portrait`.
2. `GET /v1/pdf/status/:job_id` until `status` is `complete` (with the file `url`) or `failed`.

Every attempt is saved in the report's export history, which is the list on the Download tab.

## Troubleshooting

**The PDF fails** — very large reports can sometimes fail. Try again, or spread the widgets over more pages.

**Numbers are old or missing** — refresh the report, wait for the widgets to load, then make the PDF again.

**Images or logos are missing** — check that every image in the report can be opened publicly. Private or expired image links can't be printed.
