---
title: "Create a client"
description: "Add a new client in a few fields."
---

On the Clients page, click **Add Client** to open the **Add Client** panel. **Client name** and **Website** are required. Everything under **Setup details** is optional.

## Required

<Steps>
  <Step title="Client name">
    The client's name appears on the client card, in the sidebar and on report headers. You can rename it later without changing existing reports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Website">
    Enter the client's domain (for example, `oviond.com`). Oviond uses it to make the card screenshot and to find the client's icon. The screenshot appears on the card once it's ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Setup details (optional)

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Contact first name** | The first name of your contact at the client, for example `Sarah`. Text widgets and emails can use it with the **Client First Name** variable. |
| **Client manager** | The team member who looks after this client. Defaults to **No manager**. |
| **Default currency** | The currency used by money widgets in this client's reports. Starts from your account's default currency. |
| **Reporting timezone** | The timezone used for dates and scheduled sends. Starts from your company timezone. |
| **Client folder** | One [folder](/clients/folders) to put the client in. You can add more folders later in the client's settings. |
| **Report domain** | The [custom domain](/custom-domains/list) used for this client's shared links. Defaults to **Default domain**. |
| **Client theme** | The [theme](/themes/apply) new reports for this client start with. |

Click **Create Client** to save. Next steps are usually:

1. [Connect a data source](/data-sources/client-sources). The client's card offers **Connect a data source** until one is linked.
2. [Create your first report](/reports/add): a Multi-Page Report or a Dashboard.

<Note>
Logos, the number format (for example `1.234,56`), and branding are set later in the client's [Settings](/clients/settings), not on this panel.
</Note>
