title: "Client settings"
description: "Change a client's details, logos, preview image and theme, or archive it."
---

Open a client and go to its **Settings**. The page has five sections. Click **Save changes** in a section to keep your changes.

## Branding defaults

Choose the **Logo source** for this client's reports and shared links:

- **Agency logos** — use your account's logos (set in **Settings → Company**). This is the default.
- **Client logos** — upload this client's own logos. **Refresh favicon from website** picks up the icon from the client's website.

See [Branding](/company/branding).

## Client details

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Client name** | The client's name on cards, headers and reports. |
| **Contact first name** | Your contact at the client. Text widgets and emails can use it as the **Client First Name** variable. |
| **Client manager** | The team member who looks after the client. |
| **Default currency** | The currency money widgets use. |
| **Number format** | How numbers read, for example `1,234.56` or `1.234,56`. Set separately from the currency. |
| **Reporting timezone** | The timezone for dates and for when [automations](/automations/scheduled) send. |
| **Client folder** | The [folders](/clients/folders) the client is in. You can pick several here. |
| **Report domain** | The [custom domain](/custom-domains/list) used for this client's shared links. |

## Preview image

The picture shown on your Clients list and in shared reports and dashboards. Oviond takes it from the client's website on its own. You can **Upload** your own (PNG, JPG or WebP, 1200×630 is best), **Refresh** it from the website, or **Remove** it.

## Client theme

The [theme](/themes/apply) this client's new reports start with. It doesn't change existing reports.

## Danger zone

**Archive client** moves the client, with its reports, to the Archive. See [Archive a client](/clients/archive).
